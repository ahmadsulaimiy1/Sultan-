A Word from the Founder

A Trust We Carry

Sultan Zakariya Olanrewaju Hanafi, PhD

Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

The Qur’an tells us that Allah raises those who believe and those who have been given knowledge in degrees. At Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools, we take this as more than a verse — we take it as an instruction.

Every child who walks through our gates in Ikorodu is offered two inheritances at once: the discipline of rigorous academic scholarship, and the light of the Qur’an, held in the heart through complete memorisation. We do not treat these as separate tracks. They are one education.

This edition exists to introduce that education properly — through the Hifz journey of our Qur’an College, the Arabic and Islamic scholarship of our dedicated school, and the chain of teachers, from Imam to Principal, who carry it forward with us.

“Together, let us embark on a journey where every child discovers not only what they know, but who they are called to become.”

School 03

School of Islamic & Arabic Studies

A curriculum built on carefully selected Saudi Arabian resources, SHRS proprietary texts, and recognised scholarly materials — not as a substitute for rigour, but as its Islamic expression.

Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools draws on a deliberate blend of resources: Nigerian National Curriculum requirements, international best-practice materials, carefully selected Saudi Arabian textbooks, established Arabic and Islamic scholarship texts, and SHRS's own proprietary publications. For Arabic Language, Islamic Studies, and Qur’an Studies specifically, many of these texts originate from Saudi Arabia and other respected centres of Islamic scholarship — complemented, not replaced, by materials developed by SHRS for our own students.

School 02 · Academic Distinction

Sultan Hanafi Royal College

Rigorous secular scholarship, grounded in the same Islamic formation that runs through every school at Sultan Hanafi.

Serving students aged ten and above across JSS 1–3 and SSS 1–3, the Royal College delivers a curriculum spanning seven departments — Languages, Mathematics & ICT, Humanities, Science & Technology, Commerce & Management, Arabic, and Islamic Sciences — under Principal Dr Adegoke Musa Olatunji, PhD, MEd. Established in 2021 and registered with the Lagos State Ministry of Education in May 2026, the College sat its inaugural BECE cohort the same year.
