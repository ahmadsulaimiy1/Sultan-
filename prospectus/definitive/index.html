My over two decades of work experience span banking, insurance, oil & gas, and consulting, with a bias for corporate reporting, taxation management, revenue generation, financial and business advisory, governance, and leadership. Chapter II — Heritage

A Legacy Built on One Conviction

Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools was founded by Sultan Zakariya Olanrewaju Hanafi, PhD — a professional accountant, educationist, and traditional title-holder — and officially registered in December 2017. The school's name and purpose honour the founder's own upbringing: a household of limited formal education, but of total conviction that the next generation would have opportunities the last did not.

2017
Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools officially registered.
2021
Royal College established, extending the institution into mainstream Junior and Senior Secondary education.
2026
Inaugural Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE) cohort presented; formal Ministry of Education registration secured; the campus formally commissioned before the wider community.

The campus gate, Imowonla, Ikorodu, Lagos State.

Chapter III — Doctrine

Vision, Mission & Values

Vision

To be recognised as a leading institution excelling in knowledge dissemination and character building — creating a positive impact wherever our presence is felt.

Mission

To provide holistic education by imparting both Islamic and secular knowledge through rigorous research and effective teaching methods — promoting ethical behaviour and instilling strong values in pursuit of a secure, informed, and progressive society.

Core Values

K

I

A moral compass built into daily conduct, not reserved for examinations of faith alone.

S

An education that produces contributors to society, not merely graduates of it.
