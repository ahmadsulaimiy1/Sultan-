Why It Can Be Trusted

The Record, Not the Paper

Software is only worth something to a family if it makes the school more accountable, not less.

Nothing Overwritten

Promotions, transfers, withdrawals and graduations are timestamped events, not fields that get edited. A record can be read back years later exactly as it stood.

Permission by Rule

Who may see what is decided by the school's Role and Permission Matrix rather than by whoever set the account up. Temporary authority is time-bound and expires on its own.

Checkable by Anyone

Every certificate, receipt and identity the school issues carries a reference that a third party can check against the register without contacting the office.
