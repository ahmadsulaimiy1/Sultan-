Parent Portal

A secure account for every guardian: submit and track an admissions enquiry, view your child's attendance, term results and fee status, receive school notifications, and complete a full institutional identity profile. Protected by email verification and one-time-code sign-in on new devices.

Student Portal

For Staff

Staff Identity & Permission Engine

Staff Identity & Permission Engine

Every staff member's own directory record — office, institution, reporting line, and roles — with access driven by a data-defined Permission Engine built directly from the school's own Role & Permission Matrix, plus a delegation system for temporary, time-bound authority when a colleague is away.

Registrar's Office

Registrar's Office

The system of record for a student's real academic life-cycle — promotions, transfers, withdrawals, graduations, and certificate issuance — each event permanently timestamped, never silently overwritten.

Founder Dashboard

Founder Dashboard

A read-only, institution-wide aggregate for executive leadership — student counts by status and institution, attendance and fee summaries, and Qur'an College Hifz/Ijazah statistics — reporting plainly where a figure (such as consolidated revenue or a staff headcount) isn't available yet, rather than estimating.

For Everyone

Notice & Announcements Archive

A public, dated archive of school-wide notices, with a site-wide ribbon surfacing the newest ones as they're published — see the full archive.

Morning & Evening Adhkār Centre

A full daily remembrance programme — Qur'an and authenticated Hadith texts with translation, transliteration, and sourcing, a built-in Tasbih counter, and family streak-tracking inside the Parent Portal — see the Adhkār Centre.

Personalisation Centre

Personalisation Centre

Site-wide reading and notification preferences for signed-in guardians — theme, text density, prayer-time-aware reminders, and a self-service data export, available from every page.
