Who Speaks for the School

Executive Biographies

Dr. Zakariya Olanrewaju Anofi

Founder & Head of Schools / Administrator

A chartered accountant: B.Sc. Applied Accounting (Oxford Brookes University), M.Sc. Financial Management (Edinburgh Business School, Heriot-Watt University), FCA (ICAN), FCCA (UK). Over two decades across banking, insurance, oil & gas and consulting. Before the governorship, chief accountant in the company of Engr. Seyi Makinde — who publicly credited his integrity at the 2024 unveiling of Royal College. Founded the school in 2016 in honour of his father.

Press render variants of his name (“Olanrewaju Anofi”, “Hanafi Olanrewaju”) refer to the same person; the rendering above is canonical.

Mr. Lukman Olajide Anofi

Co-labourer in the founding · brother of the founder

A chartered professional accountant (CPA) based in New Brunswick, Canada, with a Lean Six Sigma Black Belt and a portfolio spanning financial management, operations and strategic leadership. Named in the school’s account of its founding as one of those whose encouragement carried the project.

Ahmed Sulaimon

Director, Qur’an College

Director of the college behind the Sultan Annual Qur’an Competition — ₦1.5m in prizes, described in The Punch’s 2025 coverage as “the best and first of its kind in Nigeria.”
