Facility 03 · Academic & Science

Biology Laboratory

Where the National Curriculum's life-science strand leaves the textbook: dissection, microscopy and specimen study that prepare Royal College students for the practical component of WAEC and NECO biology, not only the theory paper.

Facility 04 · Academic & Science

ICT Room

Computer Studies taught on real hardware rather than a shared projector — the same digital fluency that carries a student home into the Parent Portal, the Student Portal, and the wider Digital Campus.

Facility 05 · Academic & Science

Chemistry Laboratory

Reagents, glassware and a fume-extraction bay for the titration, qualitative analysis and safety discipline behind every WAEC and NECO chemistry practical — a second view of the same working space below.

Related: Sultan Hanafi Royal College →
Facility 06 · Academic & Science

Physics Laboratory

Mechanics, optics and electricity made physical rather than abstract — the third of Royal College's three science laboratories, supporting the practical component of the WAEC and NECO physics syllabus.

Related: Sultan Hanafi Royal College →
Facility 07 · Academic & Science

Home Economics — Textiles

Sewing, pattern-cutting and textile craft — the practical, vocational strand of the National Curriculum that gives a student a skill they can see and hold, alongside every subject graded on paper.

Related: The Curriculum Architecture →
Facility 08 · Academic & Science

Home Economics — Food & Nutrition

Cookery and nutrition taught as practical coursework rather than a chapter to memorise, building domestic competence and self-reliance alongside the academic curriculum.

Related: The Curriculum Architecture →

Woodwork, metalwork and hands-on construction under the Basic Technology syllabus — the most literal expression of building something real, in timber and tools rather than only in theory.
