V — Newly Established

Online & Distance Learning School

Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools' fifth institution, newly constituted under the Head of Schools / Administrator's office as part of the governance restructuring that established the Board of Governors. It stands alongside the Basic School, Royal College, Islamiyyah School, and Qur'an College as a distinct school within SHRS, not a rebranding of the existing Digital Campus systems those four institutions already share.

In Preparation

Not Yet Open, and We Will Not Pretend Otherwise

The Online & Distance Learning School has been constituted under the Head of Schools' office. Its curriculum, faculty, fee structure and admission process are being settled now, and none of them is described here until they are real. When the Board of Governors and the Management Team have adopted them, this page will carry them in full.

Families who would like to be told when it opens are welcome to write to the school and we will keep a record of the enquiry.
