V — Newly Established

Online & Distance Learning School

Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools' fifth institution, newly constituted under the Head of Schools / Administrator's office as part of the governance restructuring that established the Board of Governors. It stands alongside the Basic School, Royal College, Islamiyyah School, and Qur'an College as a distinct school within SHRS, not a rebranding of the existing Digital Campus systems those four institutions already share.

The Fifth Institution

Constituted, and Under Formation

The Online & Distance Learning School is constituted under the office of the Head of Schools / Administrator. Its curriculum, its faculty, its fee structure and its admission process are being settled by the Board of Governors and the Management Team, and each is published here upon adoption, in the terms in which it was resolved.

Families who wish to be notified at its opening are invited to write to the school; the enquiry is entered on the register and answered in course.
