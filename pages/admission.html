Stage 5 · In Detail

The Assessment Process

For the Basic School, Royal College, and Qur'an College, entrance assessment combines a written entrance examination, an academic test appropriate to the applicant's age and desired class, and a short interview with the child and parent or guardian. Results are shared for the next step, and admission is offered into the qualifying class based on performance. The School of Islamic and Arabic Studies has no standard admission process and is open to the wider Muslim Ummah.

Stage 6 · In Detail

Every Outcome, Recorded

Every application outcome is recorded transparently in your Parent Portal account — never a phone-only or informal decision. Once your application moves past initial review, you'll see one of a small number of real outcomes.

Offered

A place is available in the qualifying class. An offer is followed by fee payment and an admission letter carrying a unique number.

Waitlisted

A place may become available. You remain in consideration and are notified in the portal if one opens.

Declined

Where a class is at capacity, the file is closed for this cycle — clearly, and in writing, rather than left unanswered.

Stage 7 · In Detail

The Enrolment Process

Once you accept an offer and pay tuition and other fees, the Registrar's Office — the same office that manages promotions, transfers, withdrawals, and graduations throughout your child's time at Sultan Hanafi — confirms enrolment and class placement, issues a class acceptance ticket, and classes begin upon its presentation.
