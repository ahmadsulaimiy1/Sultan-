Behind the Scenes

The Same Process, From the Registrar's Side

The seven steps above are what a family does. The twelve below are what the school does: the same process, seen from the Registrar's side of the desk. It is one process counted twice, and we publish both counts, because an admissions process a parent cannot see the inside of is not a transparent one.

01

You Ask

A call, a message, or a morning on campus. Nothing is committed and nothing is charged.

02

The Admissions Office Answers

Your enquiry reaches the admissions office, and a member of staff takes you through what applies to your child's age and the institution you are asking about.

03

The Form and the File

The admission form itself carries a charge, confirmed by the admissions office. You submit it with three documents: your child's birth certificate, two recent passport photographs, and a report sheet or testimonial from any previous school. That is the file opened.

04

How We Reach You

A phone number and an email address we will actually use, for the assessment date, the result, and everything that follows it.

05

The Assessment

A written entrance examination, an academic test pitched to your child's age and the class applied for, and a short interview with you and your child together. For the youngest applicants it is a gentle morning of play and observation.

06

The Result, Explained

We tell you the outcome and what it means. It is written into your portal account, and we will sit down and go through it with you if you would like that.

07

The Offer

A place in the class your child has qualified for, on the evidence of the assessment.

08

Fees Settled

Tuition and other fees are paid once the offer is in your hands, and not before.

09

Your Admission Letter

Issued with a unique number your child keeps for the whole of their time here. It is the number every later record is filed under.

10

The Class Acceptance Ticket

The Registrar's Office confirms the class placement and issues the ticket that admits your child to it.

11

The First Day

Classes begin on presentation of the ticket, and your child is expected by name.

12

Settling In

The handover does not end at the gate. A safety incident, an illness beyond minor first aid, or a substantive disciplinary matter reaches you the same day it happens.

Stage 5 · In Detail

The Assessment Process

For the Basic School, Royal College, and Qur'an College, entrance assessment combines a written entrance examination, an academic test appropriate to the applicant's age and desired class, and a short interview with the child and parent or guardian. For the youngest applicants, in the nursery years, this is a gentle morning of play and observation rather than a written paper. Results are shared for the next step, and admission is offered into the qualifying class based on performance. The School of Islamic and Arabic Studies has no standard admission process and is open to the wider Muslim Ummah.

Stage 6 · In Detail

Every Outcome, Recorded

Every application outcome is recorded in your Parent Portal account, in writing, where you can go back and read it. Once your application moves past initial review, you will see one of a small number of real outcomes.

Offered

A place is available in the qualifying class. An offer is followed by fee payment and an admission letter carrying a unique number.

Waitlisted

A place may become available. You remain in consideration and are notified in the portal if one opens.

Declined

Where a class is at capacity, the file is closed for this cycle — clearly, and in writing, rather than left unanswered.

Stage 7 · In Detail

The Enrolment Process

Once you accept an offer and pay tuition and other fees, the Registrar's Office confirms enrolment and class placement, issues a class acceptance ticket, and classes begin upon its presentation. That is the same office that will handle promotions, transfers, withdrawals and graduation for the whole of your child's time at Sultan Hanafi.
