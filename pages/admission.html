Stage 5 · In Detail

The Assessment Process

For the Basic School, Secular College, and Qur'an College, entrance assessment combines a written entrance examination, an academic test appropriate to the applicant's age and desired class, and a short interview with the child and parent/guardian. Results are shared with the parent/guardian for the next step, and admission is offered into the qualifying class based on performance. Islamiyyah College has no standard admission process and is open to the wider Muslim Ummah.

Stage 6 · In Detail

The Admission Offer Process

Every application outcome is recorded transparently in your Parent Portal account — never a phone-only or informal decision. Once your application has moved past initial review, you'll see one of a small number of real outcomes: Offered, a place is available in the qualifying class; Waitlisted, a place may become available; or, where a class is at capacity, Declined. An offer is followed by fee payment and an admission letter carrying a unique number used throughout your child's time at Sultan Hanafi.

Stage 7 · In Detail

The Enrolment Process

Once you accept an offer and pay tuition and other fees, the Registrar's Office — the same office that manages promotions, transfers, withdrawals, and graduations throughout your child's time at Sultan Hanafi — confirms enrolment and class placement, issues a class acceptance ticket, and classes begin upon its presentation.
