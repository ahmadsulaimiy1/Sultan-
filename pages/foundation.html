The Foundation Centre

Home to the Sultan Zakariya Hanafi Foundation — a non-profit and non-political organisation, Incorporated Trustees of Sultan Zakariya Hanafi Foundation, founded by Sultan Zakariya Olanrewaju Anofi and Mallam Lukman Ayinla Anofi.

6Focus Areas

Mission

Faith and Social Responsibility, Acted On

The Foundation exists to turn the school's Islamic values into direct action beyond the classroom — literacy, worship infrastructure, education, health, and economic self-reliance — for families and communities the school itself may never directly enrol.

Literacy Promotion

Promoting literacy and social responsibility through educational, counselling, and scholarship initiatives.

Awareness Creation

Creating and promoting awareness of Islamic virtues and values.

Construction of Masjids

Providing financial support toward the construction of mosques.

Educational Support

Financial support for the educational needs of selected individuals.

Medical Support

Financial and medical support to vulnerable individuals.

Empowerment

Economic empowerment and business advisory support for micro and small businesses.

Community Development

Masjids, Micro-Business, and Direct Support

The Foundation's Construction of Masjids and Empowerment focus areas are its clearest community-development work today: financial support toward mosque construction, and economic-empowerment or business-advisory support for micro and small businesses.

Educational Support

Beyond SULTAN's Own Classrooms

Financial support for the educational needs of selected individuals — distinct from the school's own admissions and tuition, this is the Foundation's separate channel for supporting learners who may never enrol at Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools itself.
