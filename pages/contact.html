When the Offices Are Open

The hours below are the hours the offices actually keep, set out to the clock rather than described. The institution is open seven days a week: the long days carry the full school day and the evening administration behind it; Thursday and Friday close earlier for the Jumu‘ah observance and the week’s close; and the weekend keeps a shorter desk for the School of Islamic and Arabic Studies and for families who cannot come on a weekday. Telephone or send a WhatsApp message before travelling and the office will have the right officer expecting you.

Three Ways to Reach Us in Writing

Each form below reaches a different desk. Choose the one that matches what you need, and the enquiry arrives with the officer who can act on it rather than the officer who must pass it on.

01

A General Enquiry

Anything not covered below — a question about a school, a programme, a facility, or the institution itself. It reaches the general office.

02

A Campus Visit

The surest way to understand a school is to stand inside it. Tell us when, and which school interests you, and the office will confirm the hour.

03

Detail in Writing

Ask for the particulars of any school, programme or facility — including current fees for the class you have in mind — and the office will send them in writing.

Getting Here

Finding the Campus

The school stands in the Imowonla community of Ikorodu, on the Gberigbe–Agura corridor, a short way off the main road at the AP Bus Stop. A family arriving for the first time should telephone or send a WhatsApp message from the bus stop, and someone will come out to meet them.

By road

Take the Gberigbe–Agura Road and turn at the AP Bus Stop into Imowonla Road. The campus is at number 15, on the left, behind the green gate carrying the school's name and its website.

By public transport

Ask for the AP Bus Stop on Gberigbe–Agura Road. Imowonla Road runs off it, and the campus is a short walk in. Telephone from the stop and the office will direct you the rest of the way.

Before you set out

Send a WhatsApp message or telephone ahead. The office will confirm the hour, tell you which officer will receive you, and have the papers you asked for ready when you arrive.
