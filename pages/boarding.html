Boarding Facility

Discover a community where friendships flourish and memories are made. Our home-like boarding accommodation provides a comfortable environment in which boarders experience cordial relationships with roommates, creating a supportive and immersive learning environment.

Boarders enjoy extra academic and extra-curricular support, more time to read and use a wide range of facilities, and the opportunity for self-reflection and discovery — including after-class Islamic lectures and post-Salah admonitions.

EligibilityCollege & Qur'an memorisation students, ages 9–16
