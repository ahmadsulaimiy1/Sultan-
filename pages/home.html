Admission

Ready to Start Your Journey

Five stages, twelve steps, one account. Every step is tracked in your own parent portal, so you always know exactly where your child's application stands — and you never have to telephone to ask.

  1. I

    Enquire

    Ask us anything, or book a morning on campus. Most families start here — nothing is committed, and there is no fee.

    A day or two
  2. II

    Apply

    Open a parent account, complete the application, and upload your child's birth certificate, photographs, and previous school reports.

    About 20 minutes
  3. III

    Assess

    An entrance examination and a short interview, pitched to your child's age. For the youngest it is a gentle morning of play and observation.

    One scheduled visit
  4. IV

    Offer

    A written offer into the qualifying class, with the result explained to you in person if you would like it to be.

    Within two weeks
  5. V

    Enrol

    Fees settled, admission letter issued with the number your child keeps for their whole time here, class ticket, uniform, and a first day they are expected on.

    Before term begins

Before You Ask

The Questions Families Put To Us Most

These are the questions the admissions office answers by telephone every week. They are set down here so that nobody has to ask them twice, and so that the answer is the same for every family.

Do you admit children who are not Muslim?

Yes. Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools welcomes Muslims and non-Muslims, sons and daughters, alike. The Islamic formation is part of the school's character and is taught to every pupil as part of their upbringing here; no family is asked to profess anything in order to be admitted.

Can an adult study here?

Yes, at the School of Islamic and Arabic Studies, which is open to the wider community rather than to enrolled pupils only, and which runs on a weekday and weekend timetable built to fit around work. It does not require the standard admission process. See the Islamiyyah →

Is boarding available?

Boarding is offered at the Qur'an College, where the memorisation programme is served by residence; the College may also be taken as a day programme. Read the boarding arrangements →

When may we visit the campus?

On any day of the week. The offices are open 7:30 am to 6:00 pm Monday to Wednesday, 7:30 am to 4:00 pm on Thursday and Friday, and 9:00 am to 3:00 pm at the weekend. We would rather a family stood inside the school before deciding on it — meet the teachers who will know your child by name, and see a lesson while it is running rather than after it has been tidied. Arrange a visit →

How long does an application take?

Five stages and twelve steps, every one of them tracked in your own parent portal so that you never have to telephone to learn where your child's application stands. The application itself takes about twenty minutes; an offer follows the assessment within two weeks. See every stage →

How do I check that a certificate is genuine?

At our public verification address, using the reference printed on the document. Every credential this school issues — results, admission letters, Ijāzah certification, staff identity — can be checked by anybody holding it, without contacting the office. Verify a credential →

Which curriculum do you follow?

The Nigerian National Curriculum on the secular side, taught to the WAEC and NECO examinations at the Royal College; the Saudi Arabian curriculum in Islamic studies, alongside Arabic taught to genuine proficiency. Both run for every pupil. Read the curriculum in full →

Where can I read your policies?

In the Governance Library, in full and without asking. Safeguarding, admissions, assessment, data protection and the rest are published as instruments with their own references, searchable by name or category. Open the Governance Library →

A question that is not here? The admissions office answers by telephone, WhatsApp and email — and every office in the institution is listed by name. See the full directory →
