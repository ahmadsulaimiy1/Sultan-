Leadership Development

Responsibility a Child Can Actually Hold

Leadership is taught here the only way it can be: by handing a student something real and letting the consequences be real too.

The roles below are not honorifics awarded at prize-giving. Each carries a duty someone will notice if it goes undone — a class that needs organising, a house that needs representing, a position that must be argued to the people who run the school. A student who holds one has been trusted, and knows it.

01

Class Captain

The first responsibility most students hold: their own classroom, and the daily business of keeping it working.

02

House Prefect

Answerable for a house rather than a room — a wider group, and older students among them.

03

Senior Prefect

Responsibility across year groups, and the first role in which a student is asked to hold peers to a standard.

04

Student Representative

The official channel between the student body and school management. The position exists so that what students think reaches the people who decide.

05

Head Boy & Head Girl

The school's senior student office, and the one a younger child watches to learn what is expected of them here.

Alongside the roles run the disciplines that make them possible: public speaking, formal debate, and creative performance — practised in front of an audience, because a position held privately is not leadership.

Welfare & Pastoral Care

Nothing Waits Until the End of Term

A dedicated sick bay, governed by the school's First Aid Policy, with a currently certified First Aider present throughout the school day. Anything beyond minor first aid goes to the school nurse immediately.

A welfare concern may be raised with any member of staff — not a designated person a child may not know — and is acted on the same day under the Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy. Guardians are told the same day too: about a safety incident, an illness beyond minor first aid, or a substantive disciplinary matter. Parents should not learn of a difficult week in a report at the end of one.
