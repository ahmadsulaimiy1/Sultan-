Our Story

A Legacy Named in Honour of a Father

Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools was founded by Zakariya Olanrewaju Anofi — a professional accountant and educationist — in July 2016.

The school takes its name from the founder's late father, Anofi Aliu Akano — a clerk at the Nigerian Ports Authority whose own education was limited, but who was determined that his children would have the opportunities he didn't. Building the school became a way to honour that legacy after his father's passing, during a period when the founder and his younger brother were serving the nation during their NYSC year.

The idea took shape years earlier, during Lagos Governor Babatunde Fashola's administration, and was carried through with the encouragement of the founder's mentor, Governor Seyi Makinde, his brother Lukman Olajide Anofi, and others. Rooted in the Imowonla community in Ikorodu, the school has grown into a byword for what focused, values-led education can do for an underserved area.

Grounded in Real Relationships

SULTAN's Islamic and Qur'anic programme is grounded in real scholarly relationships, not built in isolation — visits like these connect the school to the wider community of Islamic scholarship its curriculum draws on.
