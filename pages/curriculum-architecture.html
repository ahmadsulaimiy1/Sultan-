Two Curricula, One Standard

Basic School · Royal College

The National Curriculum Programme

The Basic School and Royal College both deliver the Nigerian National Curriculum, infused rather than diluted with entrepreneurial skills, financial intelligence, broad-based technology, personal development, career planning, and work-ready elective subjects. Royal College carries this across six academic departments — JSS 1–3 through SSS 1–3 — and leads to BECE, WAEC, and NECO. See the Basic School's curriculum and Royal College's curriculum.

Islamiyyah School · Qur'an College

The Saudi Arabian Curriculum Programme

Islamiyyah School and Qur'an College adopt the Saudi Arabian educational curriculum, with Saudi-approved textbooks and materials — Arabic language mastery, Qur'an and Hadith studies, and, for Qur'an College, the full Hifz and Ijazah pathway. See Islamiyyah School's curriculum and Qur'an College's curriculum.

The syllabus for any year group, textbook titles and publishers included, is issued to its families in full at the start of term. See our Faculty Directory for the educators teaching these curricula today.

Learning Pathways & Academic Progression

From a toddler's first day in Nursery to a graduate stepping into university, every stage connects to the next — a single, deliberate pathway, not a series of disconnected years.

Stage 1 · Ages 2–5

Nursery

Where every child's journey begins — play-based learning woven with numeracy, literacy, and Islamic values from the very first day.

Stage 2 · Ages 5–10

Primary

National curriculum enriched with entrepreneurship, financial literacy, and digital fluency — the foundation for every pathway ahead.

Stage 3 · Ages 10+

Sultan Hanafi Royal College

A rigorous integrated curriculum across six departments — JSS 1–3 and SSS 1–3 — where academic excellence, Islamic grounding, and modern pedagogy converge.

Stage 4 · Ages 9–16 · Parallel Track

Sultan Hanafi Qur'an College

A full-immersion 24–36 month programme — complete memorisation of the Noble Qur'an, Tajwid, and the Sciences of the Qur'an, culminating in a certified Ijazah. Runs alongside Royal College, not after it, for students pursuing both.

Stage 5 · Weekday & Weekend · Parallel Track

Islamiyyah School

Running alongside every other stage — carefully selected Saudi Arabian resources and SHRS-developed materials build authentic Islamic learning and structured Arabic language acquisition, open to the wider Muslim Ummah.

Stage 6 · Every Year Group

Leadership & Character Development

Guided by the CLEVER framework and a structured student leadership pathway — from Student Representatives to School Prefects to Class Captains — every student is mentored to lead, not just to pass.

Stage 7 · Beyond SHRS

Graduation & Beyond

BECE, WAEC, and NECO examinations mark the culmination of the Royal College years — opening the door to university and higher education, nationally and internationally.

Character, Leadership & Spiritual Formation

Character Formation

The CLEVER Standard, Lived Daily

Creativity, Leadership, Engagement, Versatility, Ethics, and Reliability are not aspirational statements posted on a wall — they are the actual architecture of daily school life, the same standard applied across all five institutions. See the full Mission, Vision & CLEVER Standard.

Leadership Development

A Structured Student Leadership Pathway

Every student is given a real, structured route to lead: Student Representatives — the official link between the student body and school management — School Prefects (Head Boy, Head Girl, Senior Prefects, House Prefects), and Class Captains, alongside public speaking, debate, and creative performance opportunities.

Spiritual Formation

Islamic Formation, Woven Throughout

Islamic values run through every dimension of school life — from morning routines to Qur'anic recitation and the deepening of personal faith — supported by the school's own Morning & Evening Adhkār Centre and Digital Qur'an Centre.

Entrepreneurship, Financial Literacy & Innovation

Embedded, Not Bolted On

Entrepreneurial Thinking Across Every Year Group

Entrepreneurial thinking, financial literacy, applied technology, and practical reasoning are embedded across the National Curriculum Programme's year groups — not a single elective, but a running thread from Primary through Royal College — cultivating the innovation and creativity our Mission names as a real outcome, alongside the financial literacy to manage resources effectively in adult life.
