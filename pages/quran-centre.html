A Daily Qur'an Companion

Our goal for this space: a student, parent, teacher, or ḥāfiẓ should be able to use SHRS as a daily Qur'an companion — not just a page of links. Here's exactly what that means today, and what a proper build requires next.

The reader below is a first working draft, pending scholarly verification. Its Arabic text was transcribed by an AI from memory, not fetched from a licensed source (this environment cannot reach one — see below), and has not yet been checked against a printed Mushaf. The school has asked for this best-effort version and taken responsibility for verifying every ayah before it is treated as authoritative. Please do not rely on it for worship or teaching until that review is complete.

Reader — Al-Fātiḥah & Surahs 93–114

The opening surah, plus the last block of the Qur'an — the portion most Muslim children memorise first and the one recited most often in daily prayer. The full 604-page Mushaf, verse-by-verse audio, and Hifz repeat-loop tools are a later phase (see below for why).

Why the Reader Above Is Scoped and Unverified, and What Finishing It Needs

Building the reader properly — the full 604-page Mushaf, verse-by-verse audio, and Hifz repeat-loop tools — means the verse text and audio need to come from a real, licensed data source fetched programmatically, then be checked by qualified reviewers before anyone relies on them. The version above is a deliberately smaller first step: 23 short, commonly-recited surahs, transcribed by AI at the school's explicit request and awaiting that same scholarly check. Research into what the fuller, licensed build needs:

Researched

Qur'an Text

Tanzil Project's Uthmani text is available under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 — verbatim copying and redistribution is permitted with attribution and a link back to tanzil.net. This is the standard source most Qur'an apps use.

Researched

Madinah-Style Typesetting

The King Fahd Glorious Qur'an Printing Complex's own KFGQPC Uthmanic Script font is freely distributed for non-commercial typesetting use — this is how to get an authentic Madinah-Mushaf look. The scanned photographic pages of the actual Madinah Mushaf are a separate matter and are not under a comparably clear open licence — reproducing those specific page images would need direct written permission from the Complex first.

Researched

Recitation Audio

Reciter audio hosted on sites like EveryAyah.com is generally under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial terms — usable for a non-commercial educational site like this one, with attribution, but each reciter's exact terms should be re-checked individually before enabling their voice specifically.

Blocked, Not Skipped

What Stopped This Build Going Further Today

This working session has no outbound network access to Qur'an text/audio APIs (a sandboxing restriction, confirmed by testing), so the actual verse data for the fuller Mushaf could not be fetched from Tanzil and verified here, and audio recitation isn't wired in at all. The reader's navigation architecture (surah selection now; page/juz'/hizb, manual and auto-scroll modes, and Hifz repeat-loop controls next) is built to extend cleanly once a session with real network access, or the school's own developer, replaces the 23-surah AI transcript with a verified Tanzil-sourced feed.
