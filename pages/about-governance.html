Committees

Health and Safety Committee & Complaints Committee

Two committees are currently named in SULTAN's adopted policy documents: the Health and Safety Committee, to which every hazard-related incident or near-miss is reported the same day; and a Complaints Committee of three governors, which considers formal written complaints. Further standing committees will be named here as the Board formalises them.
