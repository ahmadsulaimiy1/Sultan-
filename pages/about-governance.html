Institutional Authority

The Governance Headquarters

Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools is governed by a Board of Governors and led by a Management Team drawn from finance, education, and Islamic scholarship. This page is the single starting point for how the school is led, held accountable, and governed — the Board and Management Team below, the committees, policies, direction, and reporting beyond them.

Founder Zakaria Olanrewaju Anofi M.Sc. Financial Management, B.Sc. Applied Accounting, FCA, FCCA Chairman of the Board of Governors & Head of Schools / Administrator

A note on executive authority: the office of Chief Executive Officer no longer exists as an independent executive office. The Founder's authority is exercised solely through the two constitutionally recognised offices he holds — Chairman of the Board of Governors, and Head of Schools / Administrator — not outside the governance framework below.

Board-Level Committees
Standing Committees of the Board

Five Named Committees, One Reserved Slot

The Governance Charter fixes a minimum of five Board-Level standing committees: the Educational Technical Committee, the Finance Committee, the Governance & Nominations Committee, the Disciplinary & Ethics Committee, and the Safeguarding Committee — with one further slot reserved for the Board to name as it determines necessary. Membership and terms of reference will be published here as the Board formalises them.

Management-Level Committees
Standing Operational Committees

Seven Standing Operational Committees

The Governance Charter fixes a minimum of seven Management-Level standing committees: the Da'wah Committee, the Academic Committee, the Sports Committee, the Communications & Public Affairs Committee, the Student Life Committee, the Admissions & Enrolment Committee, and the Health, Safety & Facilities Committee (to which every hazard-related incident or near-miss is reported the same day). Health and safety matters are handled through this Committee and, at institutional level, the Safeguarding Committee — the Charter does not constitute a separate Health and Safety Committee or Complaints Committee; formal written complaints are considered through the Disciplinary & Ethics Committee.

Educators & Student Governance
The Wider Institutional Structure

Educators, Prefects, Class Captains & Clubs

Below the Heads of Departments, teaching staff report through the approved academic structure of their school. Students are represented through a recognised Student Representative body and a School Prefectorial Council operating under the approved student governance framework; each class elects or appoints Class Captains in accordance with institutional regulations; and recognised clubs and societies operate under institutional supervision. Named office-holders in each of these bodies will be published here as the relevant school year's appointments are confirmed.
