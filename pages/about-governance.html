Management-Level Committees
Standing Operational Committees

Seven Standing Operational Committees

The Governance Charter fixes a minimum of seven Management-Level standing committees: the Da'wah Committee, the Academic Committee, the Sports Committee, the Communications & Public Affairs Committee, the Student Life Committee, the Admissions & Enrolment Committee, and the Health, Safety & Facilities Committee (to which every hazard-related incident or near-miss is reported the same day). Health and safety matters are handled through this Committee and, at institutional level, the Safeguarding Committee — the Charter does not constitute a separate Health and Safety Committee or Complaints Committee; formal written complaints are considered through the Disciplinary & Ethics Committee.

The Wider Institutional Structure

Educators, Prefects, Class Captains & Clubs

Below the Heads of Departments, teaching staff report through the approved academic structure of their school. Students are represented through a recognised Student Representative body and a School Prefectorial Council operating under the approved student governance framework; each class elects or appoints Class Captains in accordance with institutional regulations; and recognised clubs and societies operate under institutional supervision. Named office-holders in each of these bodies will be published here as the relevant school year's appointments are confirmed.

Institutional Authority

Who Answers For This School

A school is only as trustworthy as the structure that outlives the people running it. Sultan Hanafi is governed by a Board of Governors and led by a Management Team drawn from finance, education and Islamic scholarship — named here in full, with what each of them is accountable for.

Founder Dr. Zakariya Olanrewaju Anofi M.Sc. Financial Management · B.Sc. Applied Accounting · FCA · FCCA Chairman of the Board of Governors & Head of Schools / Administrator

On executive authority. The office of Chief Executive Officer no longer exists as an independent executive office. The Founder's authority is exercised solely through the two constitutionally recognised offices he holds — Chairman of the Board of Governors, and Head of Schools / Administrator — and not outside the governance framework set out below. It is a deliberate constraint: the person who built the school is answerable to the structure he built, in the same terms as everyone else in it.

Transparency

What This Institution Publishes

Governance that cannot be read is not governance. Every instrument by which Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools is directed is published entire, in the words in which it was adopted, over the signature of the office that adopted it — and every credential it issues can be checked by a stranger, without an account and without asking permission.

Every Policy, in Full

Academic, safeguarding, financial and data-protection policies — each with its approval status, review cycle and revision history. Published complete, not summarised.

Our Direction

The priorities adopted by the Board of Governors and the Management Team, the order in which they are to be pursued, and the horizon against which this institution measures itself.

The Board’s Calendar

The Board of Governors convenes to the calendar settled by the Chairman at the opening of each academic year. Term dates and the milestones of the school year are published in the Academic Calendar.

Annual Reports & Resolutions

The Board maintains a register of its resolutions, from which the public record is drawn. The Annual Report is laid before the Board at the close of each financial year and published here entire, in the words in which it was adopted rather than in summary.
