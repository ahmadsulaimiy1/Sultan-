Institutional Authority

Who Answers For This School

A school is only as trustworthy as the structure that outlives the people running it. Sultan Hanafi is governed by a Board of Governors and led by a Management Team drawn from finance, education and Islamic scholarship — named here in full, with what each of them is accountable for.

Founder Zakariya Olanrewaju Anofi M.Sc. Financial Management · B.Sc. Applied Accounting · FCA · FCCA Chairman of the Board of Governors & Head of Schools / Administrator

On executive authority. The office of Chief Executive Officer no longer exists as an independent executive office. The Founder's authority is exercised solely through the two constitutionally recognised offices he holds — Chairman of the Board of Governors, and Head of Schools / Administrator — and not outside the governance framework set out below. It is a deliberate constraint: the person who built the school is answerable to the structure he built, in the same terms as everyone else in it.

Management-Level Committees

Seven Standing Operational Committees

Every hazard-related incident or near-miss is reported to the Health, Safety & Facilities Committee the same day. Health and safety matters are handled through that Committee and, at institutional level, the Safeguarding Committee. The Charter constitutes no separate Health and Safety Committee and no Complaints Committee: formal written complaints are considered through the Disciplinary & Ethics Committee. We state that plainly because a committee list that quietly omits where complaints go is worse than none.

Da'wah Committee

Islamic outreach and the religious life of the school.

Academic Committee

Day-to-day academic operations across the institutions.

Sports Committee

Physical education, fixtures and recreation.

Communications & Public Affairs

External communication and public representation.

Student Life Committee

Clubs, leadership, welfare and the life outside lessons.

Admissions & Enrolment

Applications, assessment scheduling and enrolment.

Educators & Student Governance

The Structure Below the Departments

Teaching staff report through the approved academic structure of their school. Students are represented through a recognised Student Representative body and a School Prefectorial Council operating under the approved student governance framework; each class elects or appoints Class Captains in accordance with institutional regulations; and recognised clubs and societies operate under institutional supervision. Named office-holders in each body are published as each school year's appointments are confirmed.
