Our Academic Philosophy

Faith and Scholarship, Held to One Standard

A child raised well in both faith and scholarship will want for neither. That single conviction is the reason Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools exists — and the reason we refused to make families choose between a strong secular education and a serious Islamic one.

So we built five institutions rather than one. Each carries its own age range, curriculum and learning model — a Nursery and Primary School, a secondary Royal College, a School of Islamic and Arabic Studies, a Qur'an College, and a new Online & Distance Learning School. What they share is more important than what separates them: one Board of Governors, one CLEVER standard, and a campus in Ikorodu, Lagos that welcomes Muslims and non-Muslims, sons and daughters, alike.

The result is a continuous path. A family can begin with a two-year-old learning to read and to pray in the same week, and stay with us through examinations, memorisation of the Qur'ān, and beyond — never once stepping outside a community that holds academic excellence and Islamic character to the same, uncompromised measure.

The CLEVER Standard

One Standard, Held in Six Values

CLEVER is not a slogan on a wall. It is the measure every teacher is held to, in all five of our institutions, from a two-year-old's first morning to the day a student receives their Ijāzah.

Creativity

We teach children to make, not only to memorise — entrepreneurship, technology and problem-solving woven into the timetable from the early years upward.

Leadership

Prefectships, competitions and service give students the chance to lead early — and the character formation to lead justly, as Islam asks of them.

Engagement

Small classes and known teachers mean a child is seen, not processed. Parents are partners, kept close to the life and progress of their children.

Versatility

Two national curricula, five institutions and flexible timetables let a family shape a path that fits their child — day or boarding, weekday or weekend, on campus or online.

Ethics

Daily Islamic character formation is not an add-on subject; it is the ground everything else is built on. Honesty, worship and adab are taught, modelled and expected.

Reliability

A verifiable credential system, a published calendar and a governance charter mean families can trust what the school says — and check it.
