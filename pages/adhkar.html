Islamic Spiritual Life

Morning & Evening Adhkār

A daily litany of remembrance from the Qur'an and the authenticated Sunnah, expanded here into a full spiritual companion — prayer-time adhkār, protection, forgiveness, travel, sleep and waking, and the blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ — with a smart counter that stops at each dhikr's prescribed count, Arabic recitation you can follow along with, and a session that adapts to however much time you have.

On provenance. Every supplication carried here is drawn from the Qur'ān or from the authenticated Sunnah, in the tradition of Ḥiṣn al-Muslim, and each entry is published with its reference so that any reader may take it back to the source and satisfy themselves. The collection is reviewed by the faculty of Sultan Hanafi Qur'ān College and the School of Islamic and Arabic Studies, whose scholars remain the school's first authority on questions of practice.

Every entry cited

Arabic text, transliteration, meaning, and the reference by which it is known — carried together, so nothing is recited on the word of a website alone.

The prescribed count, kept for you

The tasbīḥ counter stops at the number the Sunnah prescribes for each dhikr rather than running on, so the count is the Sunnah's and not the device's.

Read aloud

The Arabic is spoken so that a learner may follow the words while reading them, and a reader who cannot yet read Arabic is not shut out of the practice.

Kept on your own device

Streaks, attainments and the day's progress are held on the device in front of you and are never sent to the school. See the Data Protection & Privacy Policy.
