Chapter VI — Remembrance

Islamic Spiritual Life

Morning & Evening Adhkār

A daily litany of remembrance from the Qur'an and the authenticated Sunnah, expanded here into a full spiritual companion — prayer-time adhkār, protection, forgiveness, travel, sleep and waking, and the blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ — with a smart counter that stops at each dhikr's prescribed count, Arabic recitation you can follow along with, and a session that adapts to however much time you have.

A note on sources: these adhkār follow the well-established "Ḥiṣn al-Muslim" tradition used across Islamic scholarship — Qur'an verses and hadith-authenticated supplications, not a single website's compilation. References are given for every entry so they can be checked against the original sources. This is not a substitute for guidance from the school's own Qur'an College and Arabic & Islamic Studies scholars.
