# SHRS Financial Controls Policy

**Status:** DRAFT v0.1 — not yet adopted. Governs financial *process*
(approval, segregation of duties, audit) — deliberately does not touch
fee amounts, which remain unset pending real school input (see the
Tuition & Fees Policy status in the Financial Governance Framework).
**Owner:** CEO (Zakariya Olanrewaju Anofi) / Board.
**Review cycle:** Annual.
**Depends on:** Constitution & Governance Charter (Phase C, for the
Board/CEO authority split this policy applies).

> **Before this governs a real decision:** approval thresholds below
> are proposed defaults, not amounts SHRS currently applies — they need
> Board confirmation, informed by real budget scale, before use.

---

## 1. Purpose

To ensure SHRS's financial decisions are made with proper approval,
segregation of duties, and an audit trail — independent of what the
actual fee amounts are, which this policy does not set.

## 2. Scope

All institutional spending and revenue handling across all four
institutions.

## 3. Definitions

- **Segregation of duties** — no single individual both authorises and
  executes a financial transaction without independent review.
- **Approval threshold** — a spending amount above which a higher
  authority's sign-off is required.

## 4. Principles

1. Financial decisions are made by people with real financial
   competence — SHRS already has this structurally: the CEO (FCA,
   FCCA) and at least one other Board member (FCCA, CPA) hold
   professional accounting qualifications per the real governance
   roster, a genuine institutional strength this policy builds on
   rather than assumes.
2. No single person approves and executes a significant transaction
   alone.
3. Financial records are auditable — any transaction can be traced to
   an approval.

## 5. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Board of Trustees | Approves the annual budget; approves any transaction above the highest threshold (to be set). |
| CEO | Day-to-day financial authority within Board-approved budget and thresholds. |
| VP Administration | Administrative processing of approved transactions. |

## 6. Approval thresholds (proposed, pending Board confirmation)

| Transaction size | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Routine operational spend | CEO |
| Above a set threshold (amount to be confirmed by the Board) | Board of Trustees |
| Any new recurring financial commitment (e.g. a new paid service — this project has already flagged several: a transactional email service, SMS/WhatsApp Business API, real-time rate limiting infrastructure) | CEO, with Board notification |

## 7. Segregation of duties

- The person approving a transaction is not the same person recording
  it in the institution's books, where staffing allows — a principle
  to apply as SHRS's administrative team grows, not necessarily
  achievable with current staffing for every transaction size.

## 8. Audit

- An annual financial review is conducted, ideally by an external
  party — SHRS's own leadership's accounting qualifications support
  internal review, but external audit is a stronger independent check
  the Board should consider requiring as the institution grows (see
  `docs/parent-portal-phase2-3-roadmap.md` for the growth assumptions
  this maps to).

## 9. Compliance

No specific Nigerian financial/tax regulation is cited here because
none has been verified as the exact regime SHRS operates under — a
matter for the CEO's and Board's own financial/legal advisors, not
this drafting pass.

## 10. Appeals

Not generally applicable; a disputed financial decision is escalated
from CEO to Board.

## 11. Exceptions

None defined.

## 12. Review cycle

Annual, alongside the annual budget approval.
