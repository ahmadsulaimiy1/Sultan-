## 2. Purpose

To ensure SHRS's financial decisions are made with proper approval,
segregation of duties, and an audit trail — independent of what the
actual fee amounts are, which this policy does not set.

## 3. Scope

All institutional spending and revenue handling across all four
institutions.

## 4. Definitions

- **Segregation of duties** — no single individual both authorises and
  executes a financial transaction without independent review.
- **Approval threshold** — a spending amount above which a higher
  authority's sign-off is required.
- **Conflict of interest** — a situation where a decision-maker's
  personal or financial interest could reasonably influence a financial
  decision they are making on SHRS's behalf.

## 5. Policy Statement

Financial decisions are made by people with real financial competence
— SHRS already has this structurally: the Head of Schools / Administrator (FCA, FCCA) and at least
one other Board member (FCCA, CPA) hold professional accounting
qualifications per the real governance roster, a genuine institutional
strength this policy builds on rather than assumes. No single person
approves and executes a significant transaction alone. Financial
records are auditable — any transaction can be traced to an approval.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Board of Governors | Approves the annual budget; approves any transaction above the highest threshold (to be set). |
| Head of Schools / Administrator | Day-to-day financial authority within Board-approved budget and thresholds. |
| VP Administration | Administrative processing of approved transactions; maintains financial records (Section 9). |

## 7. Procedures

### 7.1 Approval thresholds (proposed, pending Board confirmation)
| Transaction size | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Routine operational spend | Head of Schools / Administrator |
| Above a set threshold (amount to be confirmed by the Board) | Board of Governors |
| Any new recurring financial commitment (e.g. a new paid service — this project has already flagged several: a transactional email service, SMS/WhatsApp Business API, real-time rate limiting infrastructure) | Head of Schools / Administrator, with Board notification |

### 7.2 Segregation of duties
The person approving a transaction is not the same person recording it
in the institution's books, where staffing allows — a principle to
apply as SHRS's administrative team grows, not necessarily achievable
with current staffing for every transaction size.

### 7.3 Audit
An annual financial review is conducted, ideally by an external party —
SHRS's own leadership's accounting qualifications support internal
review, but external audit is a stronger independent check the Board
should consider requiring as the institution grows (see
`docs/parent-portal-phase2-3-roadmap.md` for the growth assumptions
this maps to).

### 7.4 Procurement and vendor selection
- A purchase above the routine-spend threshold (Section 7.1) is
  compared against at least one alternative vendor or quote where
  practical, rather than defaulting to a single familiar supplier
  without comparison.
- **A dedicated Procurement Policy** (Governance Master Register,
  Finance & Administration, Tier 3, evaluated but not drafted in the
  Financial Governance Framework, FN-02) will define this in full;
  until it exists, this subsection states the baseline principle.

### 7.5 Financial record retention
Financial records (invoices, approval records, the annual audit) are
retained per the Records Retention Policy (IT-04), which proposes a
7-year period for this category — this policy does not set an
independent, potentially conflicting period.

### 7.6 Conflict of interest in financial decisions
- A Board member or staff member with a personal or financial interest
  in a specific financial decision (e.g. a vendor relationship, a
  family connection to a service provider) discloses it before the
  decision is made and does not vote on or approve that specific
  transaction.
- **A dedicated Conflict of Interest Policy** (Governance Master
  Register, Governance, Tier 2, not yet drafted) will formalise this in
  full institutional detail, including for non-financial decisions;
  until it exists, this subsection states the principle for financial
  decisions specifically, since that's this policy's scope.

## 8. Monitoring and Compliance

The Head of Schools / Administrator reviews transaction records against the approval thresholds
(Section 7.1) quarterly, reporting any threshold breach to the Board.

## 9. Records and Documentation

Transaction approval records, vendor comparisons (Section 7.4), and
conflict-of-interest disclosures (Section 7.6) are maintained by VP
Administration, retained per the Records Retention Policy (IT-04)
§7.1's fee/payment-record period.

## 11. Exceptions

None defined.

## 12. Appeals and Complaints

Not generally applicable; a disputed financial decision is escalated
from Head of Schools / Administrator to Board.

## 13. Review and Amendment

Annual, alongside the annual budget approval. No specific Nigerian
financial/tax regulation is cited here because none has been verified
as the exact regime SHRS operates under — a matter for the Head of Schools / Administrator's and
Board's own financial/legal advisors, not this drafting pass.
