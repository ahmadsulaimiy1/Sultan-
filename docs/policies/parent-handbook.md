# SHRS Parent Handbook

**Status:** DRAFT v0.1 — not yet adopted.
**Owner (proposed):** VP Administration (Mrs. Anofi-Badmus Fatimat
Omolola).
**Review cycle:** Annual.
**Related documents:** Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy, Data
Protection & Privacy Policy, Boarding Regulations, Student Code of
Conduct, Emergency Response Plan, Academic Regulations, Admissions
Policy, Complaint Policy (IX, live), Assessment Policy (I, live). See
`phase-b-dependency-review.md` for the full cross-reference map this
document was written against.

> **Before this is handed to a family:** this Handbook summarises and
> points to the governing documents above — it does not restate their
> full procedures, so it must be re-issued whenever any of them
> changes materially. Fee, scholarship, and international-admission
> sections are intentionally left open (see Section 12) because that
> information has not yet been provided by the school; do not fill
> them with invented figures.

---

## 1. Purpose

To be the one document a family reads to understand how SHRS works day
to day — without needing to separately read nine live policies and six
governance documents to find an answer. Every section below either
states the answer directly or points to exactly one document that has
the full procedure, never both an incomplete restatement and a link.

## 2. Scope

All guardians of currently enrolled students, across all four
institutions, day and boarding.

## 3. Definitions

Uses the definitions already established in the Data Protection &
Privacy Policy (guardian, personal data) and the Child Protection &
Safeguarding Policy (child, disclosure) without restating them — see
`phase-b-dependency-review.md` §2 for the full map.

## 4. Welcome from Leadership

*SHRS does not have a single overall Principal — it has a CEO and a
Principal (or Head Teacher) per institution. This Handbook's welcome
reflects that structure rather than inventing a single figurehead:*

A welcome message from the CEO (Zakariya Olanrewaju Anofi) on behalf of
the Board and Executive Management Team, plus a short welcome from the
Principal or Head Teacher of the specific institution the family's
child is enrolled in (Royal College, Qur'an College, School of Arabic &
Islamic Studies, or Nursery & Primary). **The actual text of these
welcome messages needs to come from those individuals — this Handbook
provides the slot and structure, not placeholder prose standing in for
their voice.**

## 5. School philosophy

Draws directly on the real, published mission and educational model
(the dual Saudi/Islamic curriculum for Qur'an College and the School of
Arabic & Islamic Studies, and the Nigerian National Curriculum
infused with entrepreneurial and life skills for Royal College and
Nursery & Primary — see `pages/academics-quran-college.html` and the
equivalent Royal College page). This Handbook does not restate the
full curriculum description; it points a parent to `/academics/` for
the institution-specific detail.

## 6. Parent partnership expectations

- Guardians are expected to keep contact information current in the
  Parent Portal — this is what makes the notifications system
  (`docs/parent-portal.md`) actually reach them.
- Guardians are expected to respond to a school request for a meeting
  or document within a reasonable window (recommend 5 school days,
  matching the appeal-response convention in
  `phase-b-dependency-review.md` §3, for consistency rather than
  inventing a different number here).

## 7. Communication channels

| Channel | Use |
|---|---|
| Parent Portal | Attendance summaries, term results, fee status, in-app notifications. Already live — see `docs/parent-portal.md`. |
| WhatsApp | Day-to-day quick communication (already live via the site's WhatsApp escalation links). |
| Email | Formal correspondence (e.g. admission, complaints). |
| Open Day | Termly, in person — broad-sheet feedback per the Assessment Policy (Policy I). |

A future Communication Policy (Governance Master Register, Tier 3) will
set formal response-time expectations for each channel; until then,
this table is descriptive, not a service-level commitment.

## 8. Attendance expectations

Attendance is tracked at term-summary level in the Parent Portal today
(a deliberate Phase 1 scope choice — see `docs/parent-portal-audit.md`,
not a gap). A dedicated Attendance Policy defining the standard itself
(Governance Master Register, Tier 2) is still pending; until it exists,
this Handbook states only what is already true: attendance is recorded
and visible to guardians, and a pattern of concerning absence is
raised directly by the class teacher.

## 9. Academic expectations, assessment & reporting

- Report cards issued at the end of every term; Continuous Assessment
  (40 marks) + Examination (60 marks) = 100 marks per term; cumulative
  200 at 2nd term, 300 at 3rd term — exactly as published in the
  Assessment Policy (Policy I). This Handbook does not duplicate the
  full grading scale table; see Policy I or the Academic Regulations
  (Phase B) for it.
- Promotion, academic standing, and certification are governed by the
  Academic Regulations (Phase B) — this Handbook points there rather
  than restating promotion criteria.

## 10. Student welfare & safeguarding overview

- A short, parent-facing summary only: SHRS has a Child Protection &
  Safeguarding Policy; any concern about a child's welfare should be
  raised with the class teacher or the Designated Safeguarding Lead.
  **This Handbook must be updated the moment the DSL role is actually
  filled** — until then, it should say plainly that concerns can be
  raised with any senior staff member, who will escalate appropriately.
- The full safeguarding procedure is deliberately not reproduced here —
  a Parent Handbook is not the right place for disclosure-handling
  detail meant for staff, and duplicating it risks the two documents
  drifting apart over time.

## 11. Boarding overview

A short parent-facing summary pointing to the Boarding Regulations
(Phase A) for the full supervision, routine, and communication
procedures — this Handbook does not restate room-check frequency or
supervision ratios, both of which live in that document alone.

## 12. Technology use

- What the Parent Portal does and doesn't store is described in full
  in the Data Protection & Privacy Policy — this Handbook links there
  rather than restating it, since getting the technical description
  slightly wrong in two places is worse than describing it accurately
  in one.
- A guardian's own responsibility: keep login credentials confidential
  (also stated once, in the Data Protection & Privacy Policy §6).

## 13. Fees & financial matters

**Intentionally incomplete.** SHRS's real fee schedule, payment
methods, and refund terms have not yet been provided to build this
section honestly, and the live admission page already discloses this
gap publicly rather than hiding it. This Handbook will be updated the
moment that information exists — until then, guardians are directed to
contact the school administration directly for current fee
information.

## 14. Complaints & appeals

A single signpost, not a restatement:
- A general concern or complaint → the existing Complaint Policy
  (Policy IX).
- A disciplinary decision about your child → the Student Code of
  Conduct's appeal process (§9).
- A grade, promotion, or academic-standing decision → the Academic
  Regulations' appeal process (Phase B).
- A data-protection or privacy concern → the Data Protection & Privacy
  Policy §11.

## 15. Parent conduct expectations

Guardians are expected to communicate with staff and other families
respectfully, raise concerns through the channels above rather than
directly confronting another family's child, and follow the Visitors
Policy (Policy VIII) when on any SHRS site. A dedicated Parent Code of
Conduct (Governance Master Register, Tier 2) will expand this section
once drafted; this Handbook states the expectation, not the full
disciplinary consequence of breaching it.

## 16. Key contacts

A directory slot for real contact details per institution (Principal/
Head Teacher, Registrar, VP Administration, and — once appointed — the
DSL). **Populate with real, current contact information before
publishing; do not invent placeholder phone numbers or emails.**

## 17. Frequently asked questions

A slot for the questions guardians actually ask most (e.g. "how do I
get Portal access for a second guardian," "what happens if my child is
sick during boarding," "how is Ijazah awarded"). **Populate from real
recurring questions the school administration already fields** rather
than guessing at likely questions.

## 18. Monitoring & compliance

VP Administration reviews this Handbook annually and immediately after
any material change to a document it references (see the Related
Documents list above).

## 19. Review cycle

Annual, or immediately after any referenced document changes materially.
