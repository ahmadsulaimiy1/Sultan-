## 2. Purpose

To be the one document a family reads to understand how SHRS works day
to day — without needing to separately read the live policies and
governance documents to find an answer. Every section below either
states the answer directly or points to exactly one document that has
the full procedure, never both an incomplete restatement and a link.

## 3. Scope

All guardians of currently enrolled students, across all four
institutions, day and boarding.

## 4. Definitions

Uses the definitions already established in the Data Protection &
Privacy Policy (IT-02: guardian, personal data) and the Child
Protection & Safeguarding Policy (SW-01: child, disclosure) without
restating them — see `phase-b-dependency-review.md` §2 for the full
map.

## 5. Policy Statement

This Handbook exists so a guardian never needs to read nine live
policies and two dozen governance documents to answer a routine
question. Every section either states the answer directly (attendance,
communication channels, key contacts) or points to exactly one
authoritative document for anything with real procedural depth
(safeguarding, boarding, data protection) — duplicating that depth here
would risk the two documents drifting apart over time, which is a
worse outcome for a family than a clearly labelled link.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|

## 7. Procedures

### 7.1 Welcome from Leadership
*SHRS does not have a single overall Principal — it has a CEO and a
Principal (or Head Teacher) per institution. This Handbook's welcome
reflects that structure rather than inventing a single figurehead:* a
welcome message from the CEO (Zakariya Olanrewaju Anofi) on behalf of
the Board and Executive Management Team, plus a short welcome from the
Principal or Head Teacher of the specific institution the family's
child is enrolled in. **The actual text of these welcome messages needs
to come from those individuals** — this Handbook provides the slot and
structure, not placeholder prose standing in for their voice.

### 7.2 School philosophy
Draws directly on the real, published mission and educational model
(the dual Saudi/Islamic curriculum for Qur'an College and the School of
Arabic & Islamic Studies, and the Nigerian National Curriculum infused
with entrepreneurial and life skills for Royal College and Nursery &
Primary). This Handbook does not restate the full curriculum
description; it points a parent to `/academics/` for the
institution-specific detail.

### 7.3 Parent partnership expectations
- Guardians are expected to keep contact information current in the
  Parent Portal — this is what makes the notifications system
  (`docs/parent-portal.md`) actually reach them.
- Guardians are expected to respond to a school request for a meeting
  or document within a reasonable window (recommend 5 school days,
  matching the appeal-response convention in
  `phase-b-dependency-review.md` §3, for consistency rather than
  inventing a different number here).

### 7.4 Communication channels
| Channel | Use |
|---|---|
| Parent Portal | Attendance summaries, term results, fee status, in-app notifications. Already live — see `docs/parent-portal.md`. |
| WhatsApp | Day-to-day quick communication (already live via the site's WhatsApp escalation links). |
| Email | Formal correspondence (e.g. admission, complaints). |
| Open Day | Termly, in person — broad-sheet feedback per the Assessment Policy (AC-01). |

A future Communication Policy (Governance Master Register, Parent
Affairs, Tier 3) will set formal response-time expectations for each
channel; until then, this table is descriptive, not a service-level
commitment.

### 7.5 Attendance expectations
Attendance is tracked at term-summary level in the Parent Portal today
(a deliberate Phase 1 scope choice — see `docs/parent-portal-audit.md`,
not a gap). A dedicated Attendance Policy defining the standard itself
(Governance Master Register, Student Affairs, Tier 2) is still pending;
until it exists, this Handbook states only what is already true:
attendance is recorded and visible to guardians, and a pattern of
concerning absence is raised directly by the class teacher, per the
Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy (SW-01) §7.13.

### 7.6 Academic expectations, assessment and reporting
- Report cards issued at the end of every term; Continuous Assessment
  (40 marks) + Examination (60 marks) = 100 marks per term; cumulative
  200 at 2nd term, 300 at 3rd term — exactly as published in the
  Assessment Policy (AC-01). This Handbook does not duplicate the full
  grading scale table; see AC-01 or the Academic Regulations (AC-02)
  for it.
- Promotion, academic standing, and certification are governed by the
  Academic Regulations (AC-02) — this Handbook points there rather than
  restating promotion criteria.

### 7.7 Student welfare and safeguarding overview
- A short, parent-facing summary only: SHRS has a Child Protection &
  Safeguarding Policy (SW-01); any concern about a child's welfare
  should be raised with the class teacher or the Designated
  Safeguarding Lead. The DSL role is now fully defined (Designated
  Safeguarding Lead Framework, SW-02) but nobody has been appointed to
  it yet — **this Handbook must be updated the moment a real
  appointment is made**; until then, it should say plainly that
  concerns can be raised with any senior staff member, who will
  escalate appropriately.
- The full safeguarding procedure is deliberately not reproduced here —
  a Parent Handbook is not the right place for disclosure-handling
  detail meant for staff.

### 7.8 Boarding overview
A short parent-facing summary pointing to the Boarding Regulations
(SD-04) for the full supervision, routine, and communication
procedures — this Handbook does not restate room-check frequency or
supervision ratios, both of which live in that document alone.

### 7.9 Technology use
- What the Parent Portal does and doesn't store is described in full
  in the Data Protection & Privacy Policy (IT-02) — this Handbook links
  there rather than restating it.
- A guardian's own responsibility: keep login credentials confidential
  (also stated once, in the Data Protection & Privacy Policy §6).

### 7.10 Fees and financial matters
**Intentionally incomplete.** SHRS's real fee schedule, payment
methods, and refund terms have not yet been provided to build this
section honestly, and the live admission page already discloses this
gap publicly rather than hiding it. This Handbook will be updated the
moment that information exists (see the Financial Governance
Framework, FN-02, for what's blocking it) — until then, guardians are
directed to contact the school administration directly for current fee
information.

### 7.11 Parent conduct expectations
Guardians are expected to communicate with staff and other families
respectfully, raise concerns through the channels above rather than
directly confronting another family's child, and follow the Visitors
Policy (SW-08) when on any SHRS site. A dedicated Parent Code of
Conduct (Governance Master Register, Parent Affairs, Tier 2) will
expand this section once drafted.

### 7.12 Key contacts
A directory slot for real contact details per institution (Principal/
Head Teacher, Registrar, VP Administration, and — once appointed — the
DSL). **Populate with real, current contact information before
publishing; do not invent placeholder phone numbers or emails.**

### 7.13 Frequently asked questions
A slot for the questions guardians actually ask most (e.g. "how do I
get Portal access for a second guardian," "what happens if my child is
sick during boarding," "how is Ijazah awarded"). **Populate from real
recurring questions the school administration already fields** rather
than guessing at likely questions.

### 7.14 Emergency communication
In an emergency, guardians receive one controlled message via WhatsApp
broadcast and/or Parent Portal notification, per the Emergency Response
Plan (SW-09) §6.6 — this Handbook does not restate that plan's
fire/medical/security procedures, only confirms guardians will be
informed through those same channels, not contacted by multiple staff
independently.

## 8. Monitoring and Compliance

## 12. Appeals and Complaints

A single signpost, not a restatement:
- A general concern or complaint → the existing Complaint Policy
  (PA-01).
- A disciplinary decision about your child → the Student Code of
  Conduct's appeal process (SD-02 §12).
- A grade, promotion, or academic-standing decision → the Academic
  Regulations' appeal process (AC-02 §12).
- A data-protection or privacy concern → the Data Protection & Privacy
  Policy §12.

Annual, or immediately after any referenced document changes
materially.
