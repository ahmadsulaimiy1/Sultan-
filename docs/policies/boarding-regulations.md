# SHRS Boarding Regulations

**Status:** DRAFT v0.1 — not yet adopted. Replaces nothing — today's
`/boarding/` page is descriptive marketing content and stays as-is;
this is the regulatory document that content has never had behind it.
**Owner (proposed):** Principal, Qur'an College (Shaykh Ahmad Ibrahim)
— boarding is primarily associated with Qur'an College's Hifz
programme, though this should be confirmed against which institutions
actually board students today.
**Review cycle:** Annual.
**Depends on:** Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy (Section 7.8 of
that policy references boarding-specific provisions this document
fulfils); Health & Safety Policy (SW-07, redeveloped in full, Phase E);
Emergency Response Plan (Section 6.5, overnight emergencies).

> **Before this governs a real decision:** supervision ratios,
> room-check frequency, and the specific boarding-house layout described

## 1. Purpose

To give SHRS's boarding population — day students' families entrust
significantly more to the school than a day-school family does — a
clear, written standard for supervision, safety, conduct, and welfare
while resident, closing the gap between the marketing description of
boarding life and an actual governing document.

## 2. Scope

All boarding students (day students are out of scope for this
document; see the Student Code of Conduct for provisions applying to
all students), all boarding house staff/wardens, and any staff member
with a supervisory role overnight.

## 3. Definitions

- **Boarding student** — a student resident at an SHRS boarding house
  outside normal school hours.
- **Boarding warden / house parent** — staff responsible for overnight
  supervision.
- **Room check** — a scheduled confirmation that every boarding student
  is present and accounted for.
- **Lights-out** — the fixed time after which students are expected to
  be in their rooms; specific time to be set by the institutional lead,
  not assumed here.

## 4. Principles

1. Boarding supervision is continuous — there is no unsupervised
   overnight period, even during sleeping hours (this is what room
   checks exist for).
2. Boarding staff-student contact rules from the Child Protection &
   Safeguarding Policy (Section 7.8) apply in full; boarding does not
   get a lighter version of safeguarding because it's "just how
   boarding works."
3. A boarding student's guardian is informed the same day of any
   safety incident, illness beyond minor first aid, or disciplinary
   matter of substance — not held until a routine update.
4. Religious and academic routine (prayer times, Qur'an
   memorisation/Muraja'ah schedule for Qur'an College boarders) is a
   scheduled, structured part of boarding life, not left informal.

## 5. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Institutional lead (Principal, Qur'an College, or as confirmed) | Overall accountability for boarding welfare and safety. |
| Boarding wardens / house parents | Overnight supervision, room checks, first response to any overnight incident, escalation per the Emergency Response Plan. |
| Designated Safeguarding Lead / Deputy DSL | Boarding-specific safeguarding oversight per the Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy. |
| Guardians | Provide accurate emergency contact information and any medical information relevant to overnight care. |

## 6. Procedures

### 6.1 Supervision ratios
- A minimum warden-to-student overnight ratio should be set and
  published (a common starting benchmark is in the range of 1:15–1:25
  for older boarding students, tighter for younger ones — **this is a
  general boarding-sector reference point, not a Nigerian regulatory
  requirement, and the actual ratio SHRS commits to must be a Board
  decision based on real house capacity**).
- At least one warden is awake and present in each boarding house
  overnight; a second is reachable within the site.

### 6.2 Room checks
- A room check is conducted at lights-out and at least once more
  during the night, logged by name and time.
- Any student absent from a room check is located immediately and the
  incident logged, regardless of how it resolves.

### 6.3 Daily routine
- Structured schedule covering: wake time, prayer times, meals, study
  hours, Qur'an memorisation/Muraja'ah sessions (Qur'an College
  boarders — see the Hifz Regulations and Muraja'ah Framework,
  Governance Master Register Phase B), free time, and lights-out.
- Exact times are set by the institutional lead and published to
  guardians, not left to vary informally by warden.

### 6.4 Health and medical care
- Per the existing First Aid Policy (SW-06, redeveloped in full, Phase E) for immediate incidents;
  any condition requiring ongoing management (medication, chronic
  condition) is recorded with the boarding house and the guardian
  informed of exactly who administers it and when.
- Illness beyond first-aid scope: guardian contacted the same day,
  medical care arranged per the Emergency Response Plan.

### 6.5 Staff-student contact
- No one-on-one unsupervised contact between a warden/staff member and
  a boarding student outside official, logged channels — mirrors the
  Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy directly, not a separate rule.

### 6.6 Communication with guardians
- Guardians are entitled to request a call with the boarding warden
  within a reasonable response window (to be set, e.g. same working
  day) — should tie into the future Communication Policy (Governance
  Master Register, Tier 3).
- Any safety incident, illness beyond first aid, or substantive
  disciplinary matter is communicated to the guardian the same day.

### 6.7 Discipline
- Boarding-specific conduct expectations (lights-out compliance, room
  cleanliness, respect for shared living space) sit alongside, not
  instead of, the general Student Code of Conduct.
- Serious breaches follow the same process as the Student Code of
  Conduct's disciplinary procedure — boarding does not create a
  separate disciplinary track.

## 7. Compliance

No specific Nigerian boarding-school regulation is cited here because
none has been verified as applicable and current — this needs
confirmation from legal counsel or the relevant Lagos State education
authority before this document is treated as regulatorily complete.

## 8. Appeals

A guardian who disputes a boarding-related disciplinary decision may
appeal through the same process as the Student Code of Conduct.

## 9. Exceptions

None defined at this draft stage; any house-specific variation should
be added as a named appendix, not assumed silently.

## 10. Review cycle

Annual, or immediately after any boarding safety incident.
