substantially deepened, per the Tier 1 retrofit directive. Every
provision from v1.0 is preserved — none weakened or removed. New in

## 2. Purpose

To govern everything about a student's academic progress that the
Assessment Policy (AC-01) doesn't already cover: how a score becomes a
promotion decision, what happens when a student is struggling
academically, how graduation and certification work, and how a student
or guardian disputes an academic decision — functioning as SHRS's
academic constitution, the document every other academic policy sits
underneath.

## 3. Scope

All students across all five institutions, in both the Nigerian
National Curriculum programme (Secular College, Basic School) and
the Saudi-curriculum Islamic/Qur'anic programme (Qur'an College, School
of Islamic & Arabic Studies) — Section 7.3 addresses where these two
programmes' progression logic must differ.

## 4. Definitions

- **Continuous Assessment (CA), Examination score, Report card** — as
  defined in the Assessment Policy (AC-01); not redefined here.
- **Academic standing** — a student's current status (in good
  standing, on probation) based on term results.
- **Promotion** — advancement to the next class/level at the end of an
  academic year.
- **Special consideration** — an adjustment to an assessment or
  deadline in light of a documented circumstance (illness, bereavement)
  affecting a student's ability to perform normally.
- **Malpractice** — any breach of examination conduct (impersonation,
  unauthorised materials, collusion), distinct from ordinary academic
  dishonesty in coursework though handled under the same procedure
  (Section 7.5).

## 5. Policy Statement

Every student's progression is measured against a consistent standard,
inherited in full from the live Assessment Policy (AC-01): CA (40
marks) + Examination (60 marks) = 100 marks per term; forms
(Diagnostic, Formative, Summative); techniques (oral questions,
classwork, assignments, quizzes, project, test, exams — POP/CBT
format); grading scale A+ (80–100) through F (below 40). This document
adds governance around that framework; it does not restate the scale
table itself — see AC-01 directly. Qur'an College and Islamiyyah
College are explicitly exempted from the CA/Examination
promotion mechanics (Section 7.3) — a named exception, not an
oversight, since their primary progression measure is memorisation and
Tajweed standard instead.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |

| Registrar | Owns this Regulation; decides special consideration requests (Section 7.6); maintains academic records (Section 9). |
| Principal / Head Teacher (per institution) | Confirms promotion thresholds and probation decisions for their institution; final-interpretation authority for appeals (Section 12). |

## 7. Procedures

### 7.1 Promotion criteria
- End-of-year promotion decision is based on cumulative term
  performance (200 marks cumulative at 2nd term, 300 at 3rd term per
  AC-01) against a promotion threshold. **The specific numeric
  threshold is not set by this draft** — it must be a decision made by
  the Registrar and Principals/Head Teacher, informed by real
  historical performance data, not invented here as an arbitrary
  number.
- A student not meeting the threshold is placed on academic probation
  (Section 7.2) rather than automatically repeated, giving a defined
  remediation period first.

### 7.2 Academic probation
- Triggered when a student's cumulative term performance falls below
  the (to-be-set) promotion threshold, or shows a serious declining
  trend even above it.
- A probation period includes a defined support plan (extra support,
  parent meeting, target review point) — not just a label.
- Reviewed at a fixed point (recommend end of the following term) to
  decide: return to good standing, extend probation, or non-promotion.

### 7.3 Qur'an College / Islamiyyah College distinction
These institutions' primary progression measure is memorisation and
Tajweed standard, not the CA/Examination framework above — governed by
the Hifz Regulations (IQ-01) and the Tajweed Assessment Policy
(Governance Master Register, Islamic & Qur'an Education, not yet
drafted), not by Section 7.1's promotion threshold. This Regulation
explicitly does not force the secular progression model onto the
Islamic/Qur'anic programmes.

### 7.4 Graduation & certification requirements
- **Secular College / Basic School:** graduation criteria follow
  the promotion framework above, culminating in a final report card and
  certificate — the exact certificate requirements (a Graduation
  Policy and Certification Policy, Governance Master Register, Academic
  Affairs, Tier 2, not yet drafted) still need to be written.
- **Qur'an College:** the credential is the Ijazah, governed separately
  and in full by the Ijazah Governance Framework (IQ-02) — this
  document does not duplicate that framework's examination or granting
  procedure, only notes that Qur'an College's "certification" is that
  document's responsibility, not this one's.

### 7.5 Academic integrity and examination conduct
- Dishonesty in coursework or examinations (copying, impersonation,
  unauthorised materials in a CBT/POP setting) is an academic-integrity
  breach.
- Examination-specific malpractice (Section 4) is handled the same way:
  investigated by the invigilating staff member and escalated to the
  Registrar. **A dedicated Examination Policy** (Governance Master
  Register, Academic Affairs, Tier 2, not yet drafted) will define
  full CBT/POP invigilation detail; until it exists, this subsection
  and the Student Code of Conduct's (SD-02) general disciplinary
  procedure govern.
- Until the dedicated Academic Integrity Policy (Governance Master
  Register, Academic Affairs, Tier 1) exists, a suspected breach is
  investigated by the class teacher and escalated to the Principal/Head
  Teacher, and disciplined under the Student Code of Conduct's general
  procedure — this Regulation does not create a second, parallel
  disciplinary track for academic dishonesty specifically.

### 7.6 Special consideration
- A guardian may request special consideration (deadline extension,
  alternative assessment arrangement) for a documented circumstance,
  submitted to the class teacher or Registrar before the affected
  assessment where possible.
- Decided by the Registrar in consultation with the relevant
  Principal/Head Teacher; not a unilateral class-teacher decision for
  anything affecting a final term score.

### 7.7 Transfer students' academic standing
- A student transferring from another school is placed provisionally
  based on their previous school's report/testimonial (already
  collected at Admissions Policy, ADM, Stage 3), with academic standing
  (Section 7.2) confirmed after the first term's real results — not
  assumed from the previous school's grading alone, since grading
  standards vary between institutions.
- **This is a new provision, not previously stated in v1.0** — added
  because the Admissions Policy already collects the underlying
  documentation but this Regulation had never stated what happens to it
  academically after enrolment.

### 7.8 Grade appeal evidence standard
An appeal under Section 12 is decided on the basis of the original
assessment record (the marked work, the CBT/POP score log) — the
Registrar or Principal/Head Teacher reviewing an appeal examines the
actual evidence, not just the guardian's or student's account of it.

## 8. Monitoring and Compliance

The Registrar reviews promotion and probation decisions annually across
all institutions for consistency, reporting any pattern of divergent
practice between institutions to the Head of Schools / Administrator.

## 9. Records and Documentation

- Term results are retained in the Parent Portal per the retention
  provisions in the Records Retention Policy (IT-04).
- A student's full academic record (not just the Portal's term
  summaries) is retained by the Registrar's office per existing school
  practice — this Regulation does not change record-keeping practice,
  only notes that it exists and should be reconciled with the Records
  Retention Policy's academic-record category.

## 10. Related Policies

Assessment Policy (AC-01, live), Student Code of Conduct (SD-02),
Ijazah Governance Framework (IQ-02), Hifz Regulations (IQ-01),
Admissions Policy (PA-05), Records Retention Policy (IT-04).

## 11. Exceptions

Qur'an College and Islamiyyah College are explicitly
exempted from the CA/Examination promotion mechanics (Section 7.3) —
this is a named exception, not an oversight.

## 12. Appeals and Complaints

**A new, distinct pathway** — not a duplicate of the Student Code of
Conduct's disciplinary appeal, and not something the Complaint Policy
(PA-01) was written to cover:
1. A guardian disputing a grade, promotion decision, or probation
   placement first raises it with the class teacher.
2. If unresolved, escalate to the Registrar.
3. If still unresolved, a final decision by the relevant Principal/Head
   Teacher — or, per the Assessment Policy's own language, jointly with
   whoever holds "final interpretation" authority for that institution.
   **That phrase in AC-01 ("Principal and Vice-Principal Academics")
   names a role that does not exist in the governance roster — this
   Regulation does not invent a Vice-Principal Academics to fill it,
   and instead names the Principal/Head Teacher of the student's own
   institution as the real, existing authority.** See the Governance
   Resolution Register for this open conflict.
4. Same convention as other appeals in this ecosystem: decided by
   someone other than the original decision-maker where the escalation
   chain allows it, within a defined response window (recommend 5
   school days), reviewed against the evidence standard in Section 7.8.

## 13. Review and Amendment

Annual, or immediately if a promotion threshold or academic-integrity
consequence is formally set (both currently open items this document
flags rather than resolves — tracked in the Governance Resolution
Register).
