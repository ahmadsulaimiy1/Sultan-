# SHRS Academic Regulations

**Status:** DRAFT v0.1 — not yet adopted. Written to function as
SHRS's academic constitution: the document every other academic policy
sits underneath.
**Owner (proposed):** Registrar, Royal College (Mrs. Anofi-Abdulkareem
Mariam Tope), in consultation with each institution's Principal /
Head Teacher for institution-specific application.
**Review cycle:** Annual.
**Related documents:** Assessment Policy (I, live — this document's
grading mechanics are inherited from it, not replaced), Academic
Integrity Policy (Governance Master Register, Tier 1, not yet drafted),
Student Code of Conduct (for non-academic discipline, kept separate —
see Section 8), Ijazah Governance Framework (Qur'an College's
certification pathway is distinct from secular-programme graduation,
addressed explicitly in Section 6).

> **Before this governs a real decision:** promotion thresholds,
> probation criteria, and special-consideration procedures below are
> proposed structure built on top of the real, already-published
> Assessment Policy grading scale — they are not a record of criteria
> SHRS currently applies. They need confirmation by the Registrar and
> each Principal/Head Teacher before use.

---

## 1. Purpose

To govern everything about a student's academic progress that the
Assessment Policy (Policy I) doesn't already cover: how a score becomes
a promotion decision, what happens when a student is struggling
academically, how graduation and certification work, and how a
student or guardian disputes an academic decision — closing the gap
between "how we grade" (already published) and "what grading means for
a student's progression" (not yet written anywhere).

## 2. Scope

All students across all four institutions, in both the Nigerian
National Curriculum programme (Royal College, Nursery & Primary) and
the Saudi-curriculum Islamic/Qur'anic programme (Qur'an College, School
of Arabic & Islamic Studies) — Section 6 addresses where these two
programmes' progression logic must differ.

## 3. Definitions

- **Continuous Assessment (CA), Examination score, Report card** — as
  defined in the Assessment Policy (Policy I); not redefined here.
- **Academic standing** — a student's current status (in good
  standing, on probation) based on term results.
- **Promotion** — advancement to the next class/level at the end of an
  academic year.
- **Special consideration** — an adjustment to an assessment or
  deadline in light of a documented circumstance (illness, bereavement)
  affecting a student's ability to perform normally.

## 4. Assessment & examination framework

Inherited in full from the live Assessment Policy (Policy I): CA (40
marks) + Examination (60 marks) = 100 marks per term; forms
(Diagnostic, Formative, Summative); techniques (oral questions,
classwork, assignments, quizzes, project, test, exams — POP/CBT format);
grading scale A+ (80–100) through F (below 40). **This document adds
governance around that framework; it does not restate the scale table
itself** — see Policy I directly.

### 4.1 Examination conduct
A dedicated Examination Policy (Governance Master Register, Tier 2, not
yet drafted) will define CBT/POP invigilation and malpractice-handling
detail. Until it exists, malpractice is handled as an academic-integrity
matter under Section 8 below plus the Student Code of Conduct's general
disciplinary procedure — not left undefined.

## 5. Promotion criteria

- End-of-year promotion decision is based on cumulative term
  performance (200 marks cumulative at 2nd term, 300 at 3rd term per
  Policy I) against a promotion threshold. **The specific numeric
  threshold is not set by this draft** — it must be a decision made by
  the Registrar and Principals/Head Teacher, informed by real
  historical performance data, not invented here as an arbitrary
  number.
- A student not meeting the threshold is placed on academic probation
  (Section 6.1) rather than automatically repeated, giving a defined
  remediation period first.

## 6. Academic standing & probation

### 6.1 Academic probation
- Triggered when a student's cumulative term performance falls below
  the (to-be-set) promotion threshold, or shows a serious declining
  trend even above it.
- A probation period includes a defined support plan (extra support,
  parent meeting, target review point) — not just a label.
- Reviewed at a fixed point (recommend end of the following term) to
  decide: return to good standing, extend probation, or non-promotion.

### 6.2 Qur'an College / School of Arabic & Islamic Studies distinction
- These institutions' primary progression measure is memorisation and
  Tajweed standard, not the CA/Examination framework above — governed
  by the Hifz Regulations (Phase B) and the Tajweed Assessment Policy
  (Phase B, not yet drafted), not by this section's promotion
  threshold. This Regulation explicitly does not force the secular
  progression model onto the Islamic/Qur'anic programmes.

## 7. Graduation & certification requirements

- **Royal College / Nursery & Primary:** graduation criteria follow
  the promotion framework above, culminating in a final report card and
  certificate — the exact certificate requirements (a Graduation
  Policy and Certification Policy, Governance Master Register, Tier 2,
  not yet drafted) still need to be written.
- **Qur'an College:** the credential is the Ijazah, governed
  separately and in full by the Ijazah Governance Framework — this
  document does not duplicate that framework's examination or granting
  procedure, only notes that Qur'an College's "certification" is that
  document's responsibility, not this one's.

## 8. Academic integrity

- Dishonesty in coursework or examinations (copying, impersonation,
  unauthorised materials in a CBT/POP setting) is an academic-integrity
  breach.
- Until the dedicated Academic Integrity Policy (Governance Master
  Register, Tier 1) exists, a suspected breach is investigated by the
  class teacher and escalated to the Principal/Head Teacher, and
  disciplined under the Student Code of Conduct's general procedure —
  this Regulation does not create a second, parallel disciplinary track
  for academic dishonesty specifically.

## 9. Special consideration

- A guardian may request special consideration (deadline extension,
  alternative assessment arrangement) for a documented circumstance,
  submitted to the class teacher or Registrar before the affected
  assessment where possible.
- Decided by the Registrar in consultation with the relevant Principal/
  Head Teacher; not a unilateral class-teacher decision for anything
  affecting a final term score.

## 10. Record keeping

- Term results are retained in the Parent Portal per the retention
  provisions to be finalised in the Data Protection & Privacy Policy
  (currently an open item there, not resolved by this document).
- A student's full academic record (not just the Portal's term
  summaries) is retained by the Registrar's office per existing school
  practice — this Regulation does not change record-keeping practice,
  only notes that it exists and should be documented explicitly in a
  future Records Retention Policy (Governance Master Register, Tier 2).

## 11. Appeals

**A new, distinct pathway** — not a duplicate of the Student Code of
Conduct's disciplinary appeal, and not something Policy IX (Complaint)
was written to cover:
1. A guardian disputing a grade, promotion decision, or probation
   placement first raises it with the class teacher.
2. If unresolved, escalate to the Registrar.
3. If still unresolved, a final decision by the relevant Principal/Head
   Teacher — or, per the Assessment Policy's own language, jointly with
   whoever holds "final interpretation" authority for that institution.
   **That phrase in Policy I ("Principal and Vice-Principal Academics")
   names a role that does not exist in the governance roster — this
   Regulation does not invent a Vice-Principal Academics to fill it,
   and instead names the Principal/Head Teacher of the student's own
   institution as the real, existing authority.** See the consistency
   audit for this conflict.
4. Same convention as other appeals in this ecosystem: decided by
   someone other than the original decision-maker where the escalation
   chain allows it, within a defined response window (recommend 5
   school days).

## 12. Compliance

No specific Nigerian curriculum-accreditation citation is made here
(e.g. WAEC/NECO equivalency, or the specific Lagos State basic-
education standards SHRS's Royal College programme should map to) —
that mapping requires confirmation from the Registrar and, likely,
external accreditation bodies, not an assumption made in this draft.

## 13. Exceptions

Qur'an College and School of Arabic & Islamic Studies are explicitly
exempted from the CA/Examination promotion mechanics (Section 6.2) —
this is a named exception, not an oversight.

## 14. Review cycle

Annual, or immediately if a promotion threshold or academic-integrity
consequence is formally set (both currently open items this document
flags rather than resolves).
