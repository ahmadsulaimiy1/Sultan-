## 2. Purpose

To govern who may enter an SHRS site, under what conditions, with what
verification, and with what safeguarding safeguards — closing the gap
between the live policy's brief summary ("visitors sign in and wear a
badge") and the full operational detail a security team, a boarding
warden, or a newly appointed receptionist would actually need to run
this process correctly on their first day, unsupervised.

## 3. Scope

Every person entering any SHRS site who is not an enrolled student or
an employed staff member — including parents, contractors, vendors,
government officials, prospective-family visitors, and emergency
responders — across all four institutions, day and boarding.

## 4. Definitions

- **Visitor** — any person on an SHRS site who is not an enrolled
  student or employed staff member of that institution.
- **Contractor** — a person engaged to perform work on SHRS premises
  under a service agreement (maintenance, construction, cleaning),
  typically present for an extended or recurring period.
- **Vendor** — a person delivering goods or services on a transactional
  basis (deliveries, catering, equipment servicing), typically present
  briefly and not requiring extended site access.
- **Parent Visitor** — a guardian of a currently enrolled student
  visiting for a scheduled or ad hoc purpose (pickup, a meeting, an
  Open Day).
- **Government Official** — a representative of a regulatory,
  educational, or security authority (a Ministry of Education inspector,
  a Nigeria Police Force officer, a local government official) visiting
  in an official capacity.
- **Emergency Visitor** — a medical responder, fire service member, or
  law enforcement officer attending an active emergency, whose access
  needs override normal registration timing (see Section 7.12).
- **Restricted Area** — any space not open to visitor access without
  specific authorisation: boarding houses, the medical/sick-bay room,
  staff-only offices, and any classroom during instructional time
  without prior arrangement.

## 5. Policy Statement

Every visitor to an SHRS site is registered, identified, and — except
for an Emergency Visitor under Section 7.12 — screened before being
granted access beyond the reception/security post. No visitor is left
unsupervised with students at any time, regardless of the visitor's
category, stated purpose, or apparent familiarity with the institution.
This policy exists because visitor access is a safeguarding surface as
much as a security one — the two are not treated as separate concerns
addressed by separate procedures.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Head of Security | Owns day-to-day visitor screening, badge issuance, and the visitors' log; first point of contact for a security incident involving a visitor. |
| VP Administration | Co-owns this policy; handles a visitor-conduct matter that isn't a security incident (e.g. a parent visitor dispute). |
| Reception/security post staff | Execute registration, identification checks, and badge issuance for every visitor without exception. |
| Class teachers | Do not admit an unscheduled visitor to a classroom during instructional time without confirming registration at the security post first — this is a real, enforceable duty, not a courtesy. |
| Boarding wardens | Apply the additional Boarding Facilities Access provisions (Section 7.9) beyond standard visitor registration. |
| Designated Safeguarding Lead (once appointed) | Consulted on any visitor-vetting question with a safeguarding dimension (e.g. a visitor requesting unsupervised contact with a specific child). |

## 7. Procedures

### 7.1 Visitor registration
Every visitor signs in at the security post on arrival, providing: full
name, organisation (if applicable), purpose of visit, person/department
being visited, and expected duration. This is logged in the visitors'
book or, once implemented, a digital visitor-management system — this
policy does not assume a digital system already exists.

### 7.2 Identification requirements
A visitor presents a form of photo identification (national ID,
driver's licence, staff ID from their own organisation) before
registration is completed. A visitor unable to produce identification
is not denied entry automatically, but is escorted at all times and
their visit is flagged in the log for follow-up by the Head of Security.

### 7.3 Visitor screening
Beyond identification, screening includes: confirming the stated
purpose against a real expectation (the person/department named is
actually expecting this visitor, where the visit isn't a walk-in
enquiry), and a basic conduct/appearance check consistent with the
Health & Safety Policy's security provisions. A visitor whose stated
purpose cannot be confirmed is held at the security post while
confirmation is sought, not waved through provisionally.

### 7.4 Restricted areas
Boarding houses, the medical/sick-bay room, and staff-only offices are
never entered by a visitor without specific, logged authorisation from
the relevant owner (boarding warden, school nurse, or the staff
member whose office it is).

### 7.5 Classroom visits
A visitor is not admitted to a classroom during instructional time
without prior arrangement confirmed with the class teacher and, for a
visit involving direct student interaction (a guest speaker, an
observer), the relevant Principal/Head Teacher. An unscheduled visitor
requesting classroom access is directed to reception to arrange this
through the proper channel, not admitted informally by the teacher on
the spot.

### 7.6 Parent visits during school hours
A Parent Visitor follows the same registration and identification
procedure as any other visitor — no exception is made for being a
recognised parent, since recognition by sight is not a reliable
safeguarding control at scale. A parent collecting a child outside
normal dismissal time additionally confirms their identity against the
Parent Portal's registered guardian record where practicable, closing a
real gap between the digital Portal (which already knows who a child's
guardians are) and the physical collection process (which currently
doesn't check against it).

### 7.7 Contractor access
A contractor engaged for extended or recurring work is issued a
distinct visitor category in the log (not treated as a one-time
visitor each day), with a defined access scope stated in their service
agreement — communicated to security in advance of their first day, not
discovered at the gate. A contractor is never granted unsupervised
access to a restricted area (Section 7.4) without the same
authorisation any other visitor would need.

### 7.8 Deliveries and vendors
A vendor making a delivery is directed to a designated delivery point,
not the general visitor entrance, and does not require full visitor
registration for a delivery under a defined short duration (e.g. under
15 minutes, to be confirmed operationally) — but is logged by name and
organisation regardless. A vendor requiring site access beyond the
delivery point (e.g. a caterer setting up for an event) follows full
visitor registration.

### 7.9 Boarding facilities access
Boarding houses are the single most access-restricted area on any SHRS
site, per the Boarding Regulations' own supervision principles. No
visitor — including a parent — enters a boarding house without the
boarding warden's direct knowledge and, outside a scheduled visiting
time, specific authorisation. A parent visiting a boarding student
outside a scheduled visiting period is met in a designated reception
area, not the boarding house itself, unless the warden specifically
authorises entry.

### 7.10 Photography and recording restrictions
A visitor does not photograph or record students without explicit,
specific authorisation from VP Administration — a blanket "visitors may
photograph the campus" default is not assumed; each instance is
authorised individually, given the Data Protection & Privacy Policy's
principle that a child's image is personal data.

### 7.11 Vehicle management
Vehicles entering any SHRS site are logged (registration, driver name,
purpose) at the gate, consistent with the live Health & Safety Policy's
existing vehicle-monitoring provision — this policy does not change
that practice, only formalises it as part of visitor management rather
than treating it as a separate, undocumented security habit.

### 7.12 Emergency visitor procedures
A medical responder, fire service member, or law enforcement officer
attending an active emergency is admitted immediately, without waiting
for standard registration — their access is logged retrospectively
once the emergency is under control, per the Emergency Response Plan's
own procedures, which this policy defers to rather than duplicates.

### 7.13 Safeguarding considerations
Every visitor-facing procedure above exists in service of one
underlying safeguarding principle: no visitor is left alone with a
student, ever, regardless of stated purpose, apparent legitimacy, or
familiarity to staff. This is not a special-case rule triggered by
suspicion — it is the default for every visit.

### 7.14 Child protection requirements
A visitor requesting one-on-one contact with a specific student (e.g.
a relative wanting a private conversation) is directed to a supervised
meeting space, with a staff member present, consistent with the Child
Protection & Safeguarding Policy's general principle that unsupervised
adult-child contact outside logged, official channels is not permitted
— this applies to visitors exactly as it applies to staff.

### 7.15 Security incident procedures
A visitor whose conduct raises a security concern (attempting to enter
a restricted area without authorisation, providing false identification,
behaving aggressively) is addressed immediately by the Head of
Security or the nearest available senior staff member, per the live
Health & Safety Policy's existing "threats professionally dispatched by
the security unit" provision — this policy operationalises that
existing commitment rather than replacing it.

### 7.16 Visitor misconduct
Misconduct (aggressive behaviour, refusal to comply with registration
or escort requirements, unauthorised photography, attempted
unsupervised student contact) is recorded in the visitor log with a
description of the incident and the response taken, and reported to VP
Administration the same day.

### 7.17 Removal from premises
A visitor who does not comply with a reasonable security instruction,
or whose conduct poses a safety or safeguarding risk, is asked to leave
immediately by the Head of Security; if they refuse, the Nigeria Police
Force is contacted, consistent with the Emergency Response Plan's
security-threat procedure (Section 6.3 of that document).

### 7.18 Reporting requirements
Any security incident involving a visitor (Sections 7.15–7.17) is
reported to VP Administration the same day, and to the Designated
Safeguarding Lead (once appointed) if it has any safeguarding
dimension, per the Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy's general
reporting duty.

## 8. Monitoring and Compliance

The Head of Security reviews the visitors' log weekly for anomalies
(repeated unscheduled visits, unresolved identification gaps flagged
under Section 7.2) and reports a summary to VP Administration monthly.
This policy's actual application is spot-checked at each annual review
against real log entries, not assumed compliant by default.

## 9. Records and Documentation

- The visitors' log (physical book or, once implemented, a digital
  system) is the primary record — retained per the Records Retention
  Policy (IT-04), which does not yet name a specific period for this
  category; this is a gap that policy's next review should close.
- Any security-incident report (Section 7.18) is retained alongside the
  general incident-reporting records described in the Health & Safety
  Policy.

## 10. Related Policies

Health & Safety Policy (SW-07, security provisions this policy

(IT-02, photography/recording restrictions).

## 11. Exceptions

Emergency Visitors (Section 7.12) are the one named exception to
standard registration timing. No other exception is defined; a
proposed exception (e.g. a recurring, pre-vetted visitor category) must
be approved by VP Administration and recorded here, not applied
informally.

## 12. Appeals and Complaints

A visitor or parent who disputes a registration, screening, or removal
decision may raise it with VP Administration; if unresolved, it follows
the existing Complaint Policy (PA-01, live Policy IX) as the general
complaints channel — this policy does not create a separate appeals
process.

## 13. Review and Amendment

Reviewed annually by the Head of Security and VP Administration, or
immediately after any security incident involving a visitor (Sections
7.15–7.17) that reveals a gap in this procedure. Amendments below the
level of a full policy change (e.g. updating the delivery-point
location) may be made by VP Administration without a full Board review;
a substantive change (e.g. altering the boarding-access provisions)
requires CEO approval per the Approval Authority in Section 1.
