## 2. Purpose

To give every student, staff member, and boarding house parent a
single, rehearsed answer to "what do I do right now" for the
emergencies most relevant to a Lagos day-and-boarding school: fire,
medical emergency, security threat/intruder, and severe weather —
rather than leaving each incident to be improvised.

## 3. Scope

All SHRS sites, all students (day and boarding), all staff, at all
times including outside normal school hours for boarding students, and
any SHRS-organised off-site activity (Section 7.7).

## 4. Definitions

- **Emergency** — any event posing immediate risk to life or safety,
  requiring an immediate, practiced response rather than normal
  procedure.
- **Assembly point** — a pre-designated safe location for headcount
  after evacuation. **Actual locations must be confirmed on-site — not
  invented here.**
- **Incident Commander** — the senior staff member present who takes
  charge until the named institutional lead arrives.
- **Post-incident review** — a structured debrief after any real
  emergency or drill, feeding directly back into this plan (Section
  7.8).

## 5. Policy Statement

Every drill produces an assembly-point headcount discrepancy report —
"everyone accounted for" must be affirmatively confirmed, not assumed
by the absence of a report. Boarding students overnight are a distinct
scenario from a daytime incident — supervision is lighter and response
time from senior staff is longer; this plan addresses both explicitly.
Every classroom and boarding room displays its evacuation route.
Communication to parents follows a single controlled channel (WhatsApp
broadcast / Parent Portal notification) so no contradictory information
spreads.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Institutional lead *(to be confirmed)* | Overall command decision during a real emergency; approves this plan; commissions the site-specific risk assessment this draft cannot substitute for. |
| Incident Commander (senior staff member present) | Takes charge until the institutional lead arrives; directs evacuation or lockdown. |
| Class teachers / boarding wardens | Account for every student in their charge at the assembly point or designated safe room; report headcount to the Incident Commander. |
| ICT Head | Sends the parent communication once the Incident Commander confirms it is safe to do so. |
| Security unit (referenced in the Visitors Policy, SW-08) | First response to a security/intruder scenario; liaises with Nigeria Police Force if required. |
| Designated Safeguarding Lead *(SW-02, once appointed)* | Consulted on post-incident review (Section 7.8) where an emergency had a safeguarding dimension. |

## 7. Procedures

### 7.1 Fire
- Immediate evacuation via the nearest marked route to the assembly
  point — no re-entry for belongings.
- Class teachers/wardens take the attendance register (or, for
  boarding, the room list) with them.
- Headcount reported to the Incident Commander within a fixed target
  time (to be set once assembly-point distances are actually measured
  on-site).
- Fire drills held at a regular interval (recommend termly, to be
  confirmed by the Board) with results logged (Section 9).

### 7.2 Medical emergency
- First response per the existing First Aid Policy (SW-06).
- For anything beyond first-aid capability: immediate call to
  emergency medical services and to the student's guardian via the
  fastest available channel.
- A designated first-aid point and trained first-aider per institution
  — confirm current coverage as part of adopting this plan.

### 7.3 Security threat / intruder
- Lockdown procedure: doors secured, students moved away from windows
  and sightlines, silence maintained, no one leaves a secured room
  until the Incident Commander gives the all-clear.
- Security unit and, if warranted, Nigeria Police Force contacted
  immediately — contact protocol to be confirmed and kept current by
  the Incident Commander/Security unit, not hard-coded here.

### 7.4 Severe weather
- Given Lagos's rainy-season flooding and storm risk: students moved
  indoors away from windows; boarding students' overnight movement
  restricted if conditions are hazardous; day-school dismissal delayed
  if travel conditions are unsafe, communicated via the controlled
  parent channel.

### 7.5 Boarding-specific: overnight emergencies
- Boarding wardens hold a current room list at all times, not just
  during drills.
- A named on-call senior staff member is reachable by phone overnight
  for any boarding emergency — this on-call rotation does not yet
  exist and needs to be established, not assumed.

### 7.6 Parent communication
- One controlled message, sent once the Incident Commander confirms
  facts, via WhatsApp broadcast and/or Parent Portal notification —
  never multiple staff members independently messaging parents.

### 7.7 Off-site and excursion emergencies
- The staff member leading an off-site activity acts as Incident
  Commander until the institutional lead is reached by phone.
- A current student list and guardian emergency contacts (already
  collected via the Parent Portal) are carried by the lead staff member
  for every off-site activity — **this is a new provision, not
  previously stated in v1.0**, closing a real gap: the original plan
  only addressed on-site emergencies.

### 7.8 Post-incident review
- Every real emergency, and every drill, is followed by a structured
  debrief: what worked, what didn't, and what this plan should change
  as a result — feeding directly back into the next annual review
  (Section 13), not held until then if the finding is urgent.
- Where an incident had any safeguarding dimension, the Designated
  Safeguarding Lead (once appointed) is included in the review.

## 8. Monitoring and Compliance

The institutional lead reviews drill results and any real-incident
report against this plan's procedures annually, and confirms the
on-call rotation (Section 7.5) and off-site emergency-contact carrying
(Section 7.7) are actually happening, not just documented.

## 9. Records and Documentation

Drill results (headcount, timing) and post-incident review notes
(Section 7.8) are logged by the institutional lead — retained per the
Records Retention Policy (IT-04); a specific period for this category
is not yet set and should be added at that policy's next review.

## 10. Related Policies

Health & Safety Policy (SW-07, the security/fire provisions this plan
executes), First Aid Policy (SW-06), Boarding Regulations (SD-04),
Visitors Policy (SW-08), Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy
(SW-01, for post-incident reviews with a safeguarding dimension).

## 11. Exceptions

None defined. Any site-specific variation (e.g. a boarding house with a
different layout) should be added as a named appendix once the
site-specific risk assessment exists.

## 12. Appeals and Complaints

Not generally applicable to an emergency plan; any staff concern about
the plan's adequacy should be raised with the institutional lead ahead
of the next annual review, not litigated during an actual incident.

## 13. Review and Amendment

Annual, after every drill, and immediately after any real incident
(post-incident review, Section 7.8, feeds directly back into this
document).
