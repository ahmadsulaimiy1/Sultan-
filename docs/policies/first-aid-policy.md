## 2. Purpose

To ensure SHRS provides adequate first aid to learners, staff, parents,
and visitors during accidental or medical emergencies — preserved
verbatim in spirit from the live policy's own stated purpose — and to
give that commitment the operational detail (who, when, how, what
record) needed for a newly trained first aider to execute it correctly
without needing to ask a colleague what to do next.

## 3. Scope

All members of the SHRS community, in school and at off-school
activities — exactly as the live policy already states, unchanged.

## 4. Definitions

- **First Aider** — a staff member trained and currently certified to
  provide first-aid response, distinct from the school nurse (who holds
  a higher clinical qualification).
- **Sick bay** — the medical room where ongoing care and medicine
  administration is managed.
- **Serious incident** — any injury or medical event requiring
  hospitalisation, emergency transport, or resulting in a child being
  sent home — the threshold that triggers the escalation procedures in
  Section 7.6 below, distinct from a minor incident a first aider
  resolves on the spot.

## 5. Policy Statement

Every SHRS site maintains trained first aid personnel, stocked first
aid equipment at defined locations, and a clear, same-day incident
reporting requirement for every response — the live policy's real
existing rules on medicine administration (original packaging, named
dosage, written parental permission required) and infectious-learner
exclusion remain in force exactly as published, unchanged by this
redevelopment.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Registrar | Owns this policy; maintains the first-aider roster and training records. |
| School nurse | Manages the sick bay, medical/allergy information collected at admission, and medicine administration. |
| First Aiders | Act as first responders to any incident in their area; apply the appropriate response; complete an accident report form the same day — all three already stated in the live policy, restated here as the foundation this redevelopment builds on. |
| Class teachers | First point of observation for a student injury or medical concern during class; escort or send for a First Aider, never attempt treatment beyond their own training. |
| Boarding wardens | Apply these same procedures overnight, coordinating with the Emergency Response Plan's overnight-emergency provisions (Section 7.5 of that document) where a nurse or First Aider isn't immediately on-site. |

## 7. Procedures

### 7.1 First aid responsibilities
A First Aider's duty on responding to an incident: assess, respond
within their training, and never exceed it — a First Aider recognising
an incident beyond their competence escalates to the school nurse or
emergency medical services immediately rather than attempting
treatment beyond their certification.

### 7.2 First aid personnel
Every institution has at least one trained, currently certified First
Aider present during school hours — **the specific current roster and
certification-expiry tracking needs confirmation from the Registrar**;
this policy states the requirement, not a claim that current coverage
already meets it.

### 7.3 First aid equipment
First aid kits are maintained at: the medical room/sick bay, reception,
the sports hall/field, the kitchen, the dining hall, laboratories, and
school vehicles — exactly the live policy's existing location list,
unchanged. Each kit is checked and restocked on a defined schedule (the
live policy doesn't state a frequency; recommend monthly, to be
confirmed by the school nurse).

### 7.4 Medication procedures
Preserved exactly from the live policy: any medicine administered must
be in its original packaging, with the student's name and dosage
clearly stated; no medicine is administered without written parental
permission on file. Allergy and medical information is disclosed at
admission and recorded in the sick bay — this redevelopment adds one
operational detail the live policy didn't state: **that record should
be flagged in the Parent Portal's student profile once the Portal
supports medical-flag fields (a Phase 2/3 product question, see
`docs/parent-portal-phase2-3-roadmap.md`, not yet built)**, so a
substitute teacher or emergency responder isn't dependent on a paper
record alone.

### 7.5 Medical emergencies
A medical emergency beyond First Aider competence triggers immediate
emergency medical services contact, per the Emergency Response Plan's
own medical-emergency procedure (Section 6.2 of that document) — this
policy defers to that procedure rather than restating it, to avoid the
exact two-slightly-different-versions risk this project's consistency
audits have repeatedly guarded against.

### 7.6 Student injury procedures
1. First Aider assesses and responds.
2. For anything beyond minor treatment: school nurse notified
   immediately.
3. For a serious incident (Section 4's definition): parent/guardian
   contacted without delay, and a staff member stays with the student
   until the parent arrives or the student is transported — preserved
   exactly from the live policy's hospitalisation provision.
4. An accident report form is completed the same day, without
   exception — the live policy's existing requirement, restated as a
   hard rule, not a best-effort guideline.

### 7.7 Staff injury procedures
A staff member injured on site follows the same First Aider response
as a student, with the addition that a workplace-injury record is kept
separately for any staff-compensation or insurance purpose — **this
project has not verified what workplace-injury insurance or compensation
scheme SHRS currently maintains; that confirmation is outside this
redevelopment's scope.**

### 7.8 Emergency transportation
Where a student or staff member requires hospital transport beyond
what a parent or ambulance service can provide in time, the Registrar
or a designated staff member arranges transport — **SHRS's current
arrangement (a school vehicle, a contracted service, or ambulance-only)
needs confirmation**; this policy states the requirement for a defined
arrangement to exist, not a specific one, since none has been verified.

### 7.9 Incident reporting
Every response — minor or serious — is logged on an accident report
form the same day (live policy's existing rule). A serious incident is
additionally reported to the Registrar and, where it has any
safeguarding dimension (e.g. an injury pattern suggesting neglect or
abuse), to the Designated Safeguarding Lead per the Child Protection &
Safeguarding Policy's general reporting duty.

### 7.10 Parent notification
A parent is contacted the same day for any incident beyond minor
first aid, and immediately for a serious incident — consistent with
the live policy's hospitalisation-notification rule, extended here to
cover the full range of "beyond minor" incidents, not hospitalisation
alone.

### 7.11 Record keeping
Accident report forms are retained per the Records Retention Policy
(IT-04) — that policy does not yet name a specific period for first-
aid/medical incident records specifically; this is a gap for that
policy's next review to close, named here rather than silently
assumed.

### 7.12 Serious incident escalation
A serious incident (Section 4) is escalated the same day to the
Registrar, and to the relevant Principal/Head Teacher where it involves
a student, per the general escalation principle this whole document
ecosystem uses consistently (an independent, more senior reviewer
informed promptly, not after the fact).

### 7.13 Training requirements
Every First Aider maintains current first-aid certification, renewed
per the certifying body's own schedule (typically every 1–3 years,
depending on the certification standard used) — **SHRS's specific
current certification provider and renewal schedule need confirmation
from the Registrar**; this policy states the requirement for currency
to be tracked, not a specific provider or interval, since none has been
verified.

## 8. Monitoring and Compliance

The Registrar reviews accident report form completeness and First
Aider certification currency at least termly, reporting a summary to
VP Administration.

## 9. Records and Documentation

Accident report forms, First Aider certification records, and the
medicine-administration permission forms collected at admission are
the primary records this policy generates — retained per the Records
Retention Policy (IT-04), with the specific period for this category
still an open item for that policy's next review.

## 10. Related Policies

Health & Safety Policy (SW-07), Emergency Response Plan (SW-09, medical-
emergency and overnight-emergency procedures this policy defers to),
Child Protection & Safeguarding Policy (SW-01, for any incident with a
safeguarding dimension), Data Protection & Privacy Policy (IT-02, for
medical/allergy record handling).

## 11. Exceptions

None defined; a student with a chronic condition requiring an
individualised care plan beyond standard First Aider response should
have that plan documented separately by the school nurse, referenced
here rather than treated as an undocumented informal exception.

## 12. Appeals and Complaints

A parent disputing how an incident was handled raises it through the
existing Complaint Policy (PA-01, live Policy IX) — this policy does
not create a separate channel.

## 13. Review and Amendment

Annual, or immediately after any serious incident that reveals a gap in
this procedure. **This review is already overdue** (the live policy's
last stated review date, July 2025, has passed) — the first action
upon adopting this redeveloped version should be to actually complete
that overdue review, not simply reset the date forward.
