# SHRS Financial Governance Framework

**Status:** DRAFT v0.1 — not yet adopted. Ties together the one
financial policy already drafted in full and evaluates the remaining
named ones — most of which are genuinely blocked on real data SHRS
hasn't provided, not on drafting effort.
**Owner:** Head of Schools / Administrator (Zakaria Olanrewaju Anofi) / Board.
**Review cycle:** Annual.
**Depends on:** Financial Controls Policy (Phase C, already drafted in
full); Admissions Policy (Phase B, which already names the same fee
gap).

---

## 1. Purpose

To confirm what financial governance is already in place (approval
process, segregation of duties) and to state plainly why the remaining
named financial policies cannot yet be drafted honestly — because they
require real figures the school has not provided, not because of any
remaining drafting effort.

## 2. Already drafted in full

| Document | Status |
|---|---|
| Financial Controls Policy | Drafted, Phase C — governs approval process and segregation of duties independent of actual fee amounts |

## 3. Evaluation of remaining named items

| Policy | Needed now? | Reasoning | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tuition & Fees Policy** | **Blocked, not deferred by choice** | The live admission page already discloses publicly that fee figures aren't published yet. This project has refused, consistently, to invent them. This is the single real prerequisite before this policy — or the Parent Handbook's fees section, or the Admissions Policy's fee references — can be completed honestly. | 1 |
| **Refund Policy** | Blocked | Explicitly depends on the Tuition & Fees Policy (Governance Master Register) — cannot be meaningfully drafted before it. | 2 |
| **Procurement Policy** | Not urgent | No indication of a current procurement problem; the Financial Controls Policy's segregation-of-duties principle already covers the core risk at current scale. | 3 |
| **Scholarship Governance Framework** | Blocked | The live admission page discloses that scholarship criteria aren't published yet — same honesty constraint as the Tuition & Fees Policy; a scholarship governance framework without criteria to govern would be an empty shell. | 3 |

## 4. Recommendation

**The single highest-value next step in SHRS's entire financial
governance picture is not a drafting task — it's the school providing
its real fee schedule.** Once that exists, the Tuition & Fees Policy,
Refund Policy, and Scholarship Governance Framework can all be drafted
in the same real, substantive way as every other document in this set
— and the Parent Handbook, Admissions Policy, and live admission page
can all be completed honestly instead of carrying an open placeholder.
Until then, drafting these would mean inventing figures, which this
project has refused to do throughout.

## 5. Review cycle

Annual, or immediately once real fee/scholarship figures are provided.
