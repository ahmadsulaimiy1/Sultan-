### ACTIVATION OF YOUR OFFICIAL PORTAL ACCOUNT — OFFICE OF THE REGISTRAR

Dear **Madam**,

I am pleased to inform you that your official account on the Sultan
Hanafi Royal Schools Digital Campus has been created, and that the
Office of the Registrar is now open to you online.

Your Staff Identity Number is **SHRS-HQ-REG-130826-000004**. This
number is issued by the institution, is permanent, and identifies you on
every record, certificate and verification the school produces. Please
keep it.

### What you need to do

**1. Open your activation link.**

> **[paste the activation link here]**

This link is for you alone. It can be used **once**, and it expires
**seven days** from the date of this letter. If it lapses before you use
it, simply ask this office for another; nothing is lost.

**2. Choose your password.**

The link opens a page asking you to set a password. It must be at least
**ten characters**. Choose something only you would think of, and do not
reuse a password you use elsewhere.

**No one at the school sets your password, and no one can see it** —
not this office, not the ICT Office, not the system administrator. If
you forget it, you will be sent a fresh link to choose a new one. Please
therefore do not write it down where others can find it, and do not
share it with anyone, including anyone claiming to be from the school.

**3. Sign in.**

Go to **https://shroyalschools.com/portal/staff/login/** and sign in with
your email address and the password you have just chosen.

For your security, a one-time code may be sent to your email address at
sign-in. It is valid for **ten minutes**, and you have **five attempts**
before the code must be re-sent.

### What your portal opens

As Registrar you have been granted the `REG` authority, which carries
the responsibilities of your office into the system:

- **The Registrar's Office** — admissions verification, enrolment,
  results, transcripts and certificates
- **The Newsroom** — writing and publishing the school's announcements
  to the website and to families
- **Your Desk** — messages from families who have asked to speak with a
  person rather than the assistant, and notifications addressed to you
- **Examination readiness** — WAEC and NECO oversight
- **Delegation** — if you travel or take leave, you may pass your
  authority to a named colleague for a stated period, with a reason. It
  ends by itself on the date you set; you need not remember to withdraw
  it.

### Also now open to your office

Since your account was prepared, several parts of the Digital Campus that
belong to the Registry have been completed:

- **The Graduation Control Centre** — graduation is a recorded chain, and
  your office begins it. Marking a record *under review* or *verified*
  opens the clearance chain; the Principal of the candidate's own
  institution then acts at the principal stage, and you retain visibility
  of the whole chain.
- **The Certificate Generation Centre** — certificates and transcripts are
  generated from the record, each carrying a verification address.
- **Your digital signature** — the one item requiring an action from you
  before certificates and transcripts can be issued at all. If a required
  signatory has no signature on file, generation stops with a named
  vacancy rather than printing a blank signature line.
- **Examination readiness** — WAEC and NECO, held jointly with Academic
  Affairs.
- **Admissions verification** — verification and the waiting list.
- **Corrections, and the record of them** — attendance and assessment
  corrections are logged against your name.
- **The Newsroom** — school-wide academic notices.
- **Delegation** — authority passed to a named colleague for a stated
  period, expiring by itself.

### What this office suggests you do first

Sign in, upload your signature, and open the Graduation Control Centre
once. Those three unblock everything else.


### A note on the record

Every action taken in the portal is recorded against the person who took
it. This is not surveillance; it is the ordinary discipline of an
institution that issues certificates and holds children's records, and
it protects you as much as it protects the school. It is also why your
account is yours alone and must never be shared with a colleague,
however convenient it might seem — an action taken on your account is
recorded as yours.

### If anything does not work

Please do not attempt a workaround. Write to
**[info@shroyalschools.com / the ICT Office]** or to this office
directly, stating what you were doing and what the screen said. A
message that quotes the exact wording on screen is resolved far faster
than one that does not.

Please confirm to this office once you have signed in successfully.
