# SHRS Website Excellence Roadmap v1.0

**Scope.** The public-facing marketing website only — homepage, admissions,
academics, facilities, governance/policies, communications, mobile
experience. Explicitly **not** in scope, per this directive: new portals,
new modules, new documents beyond this one, or speculative features. This
is a prioritisation instrument for improving what already exists, written
while Cloudflare/Neon deployment work is paused.

**Method.** This is not a re-audit from zero. `docs/public-website-
maturity-assessment.md` (this same engagement, most recent full scorecard)
already did the detailed, evidence-based scoring of ten categories against
real files and real screenshots — that document remains the source of
record for *why* each score is what it is. This roadmap does three new
things instead of repeating that work: (1) re-reads the site fresh against
nine specific visitor personas and a direct prestige benchmark, both asked
for explicitly in this directive and not previously done in that form;
(2) adds a faculty-presentation *design standard* recommendation, since no
real staff directory exists yet to audit; (3) converts every open finding
across both documents into one ranked, categorised action list. Every
factual claim below was re-verified against the live source files this
session (admission.html, academics.html, facilities.html, about.html, and
the placeholder/image-slot inventory) — nothing here is copied forward
unchecked.

**Standing discipline, unchanged from every other document in this
project:** no invented staff names, fees, dates, or credentials appear
anywhere below. Where the honest answer is "the school needs to supply
this," that is stated plainly rather than filled in.

## Part 1 — Nine-Persona Walkthrough

Each persona below is scored on one question: **"Would this website give
me what I need to act — enrol, apply, donate, approve, partner, or
certify — without leaving the site or making a phone call first?"**

### 1. Prospective parent (local, Lagos/Ikorodu)
**Gets:** a clear hero CTA ("Enrol Your Ward"), a real 12-stage admissions
process, real campus photos, real contact channels (WhatsApp/call/email),
an embedded map, and unusually deep safeguarding/governance policy
disclosure for a school this size.
**Doesn't get:** the one number every parent asks first — **tuition fees**
— which is honestly marked "Content Needed From SULTAN" rather than
invented. This is still the single highest-value content gap on the
entire site.
**Verdict:** Would enquire. Would not be able to budget or compare without
a phone call.

### 2. Prospective student (older secondary / Qur'an College age)
**Gets:** real Hifz Journey stages, Ijazah framework, a working Student
Portal (once staff-issued), facilities photography aimed at daily life
(sick bay, recreation ground, CCA room).
**Doesn't get:** any first-person "student life" content — clubs,
competitions, leadership opportunities, peer testimonials. Everything a
16-year-old would actually want to picture themselves doing on this
campus is currently written for the parent reading over their shoulder,
not for them.
**Verdict:** Informed about structure, not excited about experience.

### 3. Donor / sponsor
**Gets:** a real founding story with an independent, third-party citation
(Punch Newspapers, 2023) — a genuinely rare, credible asset most school
sites don't have — plus a named, credentialed Director and a public
Governance page.
**Doesn't get:** any donor-facing page at all. No named giving levels, no
capital-project wishlist, no "what your gift funds" framing, no
foundation/endowment page beyond the general `/foundation/` institutional
content. A donor has nowhere obvious to click.
**Verdict:** Would trust the institution; has no path to give.

### 4. Regulator (Lagos State education authority, in this reviewer's
judgment of what such a reviewer checks first)
**Gets:** safeguarding policy at real 13-section depth, a Constitution &
Governance Charter, a named Board.
**Doesn't get:** the single fact a regulator checks first — a
**registration/RC number and the name of the registering authority.** The
site states "officially registered in December 2017" with no verifiable
identifier anywhere. This is the sharpest, most fixable trust gap on the
whole site, and it is a documentation problem, not a design one.
**Verdict:** Reads as legitimate; cannot be independently verified from
the site alone.

### 5. Educational partner (another school, an exam board, a curriculum
vendor)
**Gets:** a described Nigerian-curriculum secondary offering (Royal
College) and a distinct Qur'an College/Islamic Studies track.
**Doesn't get:** any stated examination-body affiliation (WAEC/NECO or
equivalent) for Royal College, despite the site describing
Nigerian-curriculum secondary education. A partner cannot confirm exam
pathway compatibility from the site.
**Verdict:** Structurally plausible, not independently confirmable.

### 6. Saudi parent (GCC-aligned expectations: Arabic fluency, Islamic
authenticity, examinable Qur'an credentialing)
**Gets:** a full Arabic mirror of the site, a real Hifz/Ijazah system with
named stages and a permanent register, manuscript/geometric visual
styling distinct to Qur'an College — genuinely unusual depth for this
category (scored 9/10, Exemplary, in the maturity assessment, the site's
single strongest category).
**Doesn't get:** full confidence in the Arabic text itself — the Arabic
homepage carries its own honest disclosure that translations should be
reviewed by a native speaker, especially names and Shar'i terminology. A
GCC parent reading closely will notice that caveat.
**Verdict:** The closest this site comes to "GCC-aligned institution"
already — held back only by an admitted, temporary translation-quality
gap, not by substance.

### 7. British educator (expects: transparent curriculum mapping, staff
qualifications visible, inspection-style documentation)
**Gets:** the Director's own credentials are named and checkable (Oxford
Brookes, Heriot-Watt, FCCA, FCA); 20+ operational-depth policies with
named preparer/reviewer/approver per document — closer to a UK
independent-school governance standard than most West African school
sites attempt.
**Doesn't get:** any visible teaching-staff qualifications. There is
currently no public faculty directory at all (by design — no invented
names or credentials have been published; see Part 3).
**Verdict:** Governance impresses; teaching-staff transparency is
entirely absent, correctly so given no real data exists yet to publish.

### 8. School inspector (audits presence, not persuasion)
**Gets:** exactly the checklist above under "Regulator," plus one more
inspector-specific item: a genuine Emergency Response Plan, First Aid
Policy, and Health & Safety Policy all published at full operational
depth, not summarised.
**Doesn't get:** any visible inspection/accreditation history — no
mention of a prior inspection, a national accreditation body, or a
compliance certificate. May be genuinely true that none exists yet; if
one does, it isn't on the site.
**Verdict:** Policy readiness is strong; inspection/accreditation history
is either absent or unpublished.

### 9. University admissions officer (evaluating a transcript years later,
per IQ-02's own "verifiable years later" design intent)
**Gets:** a published Ijazah Governance Framework describing a permanent
register with revocation/annotation rules — the right *policy* shape for
a credential a university would eventually need to trust.
**Doesn't get:** any live, public verification mechanism yet. IQ-02 §7.5's
own text names a future public verification endpoint as separate,
not-yet-built work — this roadmap doesn't re-scope it (it is out of this
directive's "no new portals/features" boundary) but flags it as the one
open item this persona would actually need.
**Verdict:** The policy exists to make this trustworthy eventually;
nothing to check today.

## Part 2 — Prestige Benchmark

**Would this currently read as a leading Nigerian private school?**
Yes, comfortably. The visual system (Cinzel/Amiri display type, a real
expanded colour palette, motion design used with restraint, a coherent
crest and seal narrative), the depth of published governance, and the
mega-menu/footer information architecture exceed what most Nigerian
private-school websites in this segment publish.

**A leading West African Islamic school?**
Yes — the Islamic Identity category is this site's strongest asset
(9/10 in the maturity assessment): a real Hifz Journey, Ijazah register,
Adhkār Centre with genuine Qur'an/Hadith sourcing, and manuscript-styled
Qur'an College presentation. Few peer institutions in this category build
anything close to this depth digitally.

**A leading GCC-aligned educational institution?**
Not yet, and the gap is specific, not vague: GCC-aligned parents expect
(a) fully-verified, native-quality Arabic — this site discloses its own
Arabic as provisional; (b) visible fee transparency and scholarship
criteria — currently absent; (c) named accreditation/examination-body
affiliation — currently absent. All three are fixable with real
information the school already has (or can obtain), not further design
work.

**A future international college?**
Structurally aspirational, not yet substantiated. The Ijazah
Governance Framework's "verifiable years later" design and the
Constitution & Governance Charter both point the right direction, but an
international-college-grade site needs the registration/accreditation
proof, published tuition, and (eventually) a real faculty directory —
none of which is a design problem; all three are documentation the
school must supply.

**Honest one-line summary:** this site already outperforms its most
direct local and regional peers on production values and governance
transparency; every gap standing between it and the next tier up is a
missing *fact* (a number, a name, a certificate), not a missing feature.

## Part 3 — Faculty Presentation Standard (recommendation, not a build)

No real staff data exists yet — `docs/workforce-data-collection-request.md`
is still an open collection request, and this directive explicitly
excludes building new modules. What follows is the **structure to apply
the moment real data arrives**, so that work doesn't require its own
design pass later:

- **Directory structure:** group by institution (Royal College / Qur'an
  College / Islamic & Arabic Studies / Nursery & Primary), matching the
  site's existing four-path framing — not one undifferentiated staff
  list.
- **Profile standard, per person:** full name, title, real qualification
  (degree + institution, matching the Director's-message precedent of
  naming real credentials), subject/class assignment, one sentence of
  real biography if supplied — never a stock "passionate educator"
  placeholder line.
- **Qualification display:** plain text, not badges/icons implying
  third-party certification unless a real certifying body is named.
- **Department display:** Qur'an College and Islamic & Arabic Studies
  faculty should not be merged into one list — the workforce collection
  request itself already establishes them as distinct.
- **What not to do:** do not publish a directory with any placeholder
  rows, silhouette avatars implying "photo coming soon" for named
  individuals, or invented qualifications — the same anti-fabrication
  standard already applied everywhere else on this site.

## Part 4 — The Excellence Roadmap, Categorised

### Critical (Must Do)
1. Publish the real tuition fee schedule (or a clear "fees available on
   enquiry" statement if the school genuinely isn't ready to publish
   numbers — but say that deliberately, not by omission).
2. Publish the school's registration/RC number and the name of the
   registering authority.
3. Confirm and publish any real examination-body affiliation (WAEC/NECO
   or equivalent) for Royal College, if one exists.

### High Impact (Should Do)
4. Build a real online admissions application (Stage 2) once Staff
   Identity's Registrar role can process it — currently every stage past
   Enquiry is descriptive text only.
5. Publish scholarship criteria, if a real, approved policy exists.
6. Add a donor/sponsor-facing page: named giving levels or a capital-
   project wishlist, once the school defines them.
7. Add real student-life content aimed at the student reader, not the
   parent (clubs, competitions, leadership roles) — using only real,
   already-occurred activities (the site already has workshop and
   competition photography to draw on).
8. Get Staff Identity's Communications role publishing through the
   already-built announcement system — the infrastructure is real but
   currently holds zero live announcements.
9. Surface the Punch Newspapers third-party citation more prominently
   (e.g., a small "As featured in" mention near the homepage hero) once
   the real article URL is confirmed.
10. Commission a native-Arabic-speaker review pass on the flagged
    categories (names, Shar'i terminology) before treating the Arabic
    site as production-final.
11. Give Nursery & Primary and Islamic & Arabic Studies campus photography
    to replace their remaining `.image-slot` placeholders (Foundation
    programme photography is the third open slot).
12. Publish a real Academic Calendar once term dates are supplied,
    replacing the current honest placeholder.
13. Run one real Lighthouse pass (homepage + two deep pages) — Performance
    is currently an *unaudited estimate*, not a measured score.
14. Run one real accessibility pass (axe-core or Lighthouse's
    accessibility pane) — same caveat, currently unaudited.
15. Add a lightweight breadcrumb component to three-plus-level pages
    (Academics sub-pages, Policy detail pages).

### Nice to Have
16. Add a PWA manifest (install-to-homescreen, basic offline shell) for
    the portal dashboards.
17. Run a dedicated mobile-viewport Playwright pass over the Parent/
    Student/Founder portal dashboards specifically (the marketing pages
    already have this; the portals don't yet).
18. Scope `js/adhkar-data.js`/`js/adhkar-app.js` script tags to only the
    pages that use them, cutting unnecessary payload site-wide.
19. Add a responsive `srcset` pass across the campus photo gallery.
20. Confirm `font-display` strategy on the Google Fonts `<link>` to avoid
    render-blocking.
21. Any real inspection/accreditation history the school holds — publish
    once confirmed to exist.

### Future Expansion (explicitly out of this directive's scope, named so
they aren't silently dropped)
22. A language selector UI — defer until a second non-Arabic translation
    actually exists to select between.
23. Full body-text search indexing (currently title/description-only).
24. IQ-02 §7.5's public, third-party Ijazah verification endpoint — its
    own access-control model, separate future work.
25. Full real faculty directory build-out, once workforce data (Part 3
    above) is actually supplied.

## What this roadmap is not

It is not a rebuild instruction — most items above are content or
documentation gaps the school must close, not code the assistant can
write from imagination. It is not a claim that the site currently lags
its peers — Part 2's benchmark says plainly it doesn't, in two of four
tiers. And it is not a substitute for `docs/public-website-maturity-
assessment.md`'s per-category scoring — that remains the detailed record;
this document exists to turn it, plus the two new lenses this directive
asked for (persona walkthrough, prestige benchmark), into one ranked,
actionable list.

**Resume point:** when you say "Let's continue Cloudflare," the next
step is exactly where paused — Phase 3 (Database Initialization) of the
Digital Campus go-live roadmap, via the browser-console `fetch()` call to
`/api/portal/setup` already provided, with Phases 4–6 (live user-journey
verification, portal verification, Operational Readiness Report)
following in order. No re-audit of the Cloudflare/Neon work is needed —
Phases 1–2 are already confirmed done.
