# SHRS Governance Resolution Register

*A living tracking register — distinct from the Governance Master
Register, which tracks which **documents** exist. This one tracks
unresolved **decisions** scattered across those documents: the "needs
Board confirmation," "needs legal review," and "pending real school
input" notes that appear throughout the 23 policy documents drafted so
far. Nothing here is new content — it is every open item already named
somewhere else, collected in one place so none of them quietly get
forgotten inside a 200-line policy document.*

**Owner:** Head of Schools / Administrator, on the Board's behalf — this
register itself is reviewed by the Board. *(Updated 2026-08-04: this
row previously read "CEO," before the Board's governance restructuring
amendment of that date abolished the office of Chief Executive Officer
and established the office of Head of Schools / Administrator in its
place — see Category 8 below.)*
**Review cycle:** Quarterly (more frequent than the annual policy
review cycle, since resolution items are meant to close, not sit).

## How to read this

- **Status:** `OPEN` (not yet actioned), `IN PROGRESS`, `BLOCKED`
  (waiting on something outside SHRS's control to resolve, e.g. a
  professional's confirmation), `RESOLVED`.
- **Priority:** Critical / High / Medium / Low — independent of the
  source document's Tier, since a Tier 3 document can still contain a
  Critical open item (e.g. a safeguarding-adjacent detail).
- **Approval pathway:** who actually signs off once the item is
  resolved — not who does the underlying work.

**The three Critical items are, in order of true dependency:** appoint
a Designated Safeguarding Lead → confirm real external referral agency
contacts (which the DSL should do personally, not this register) →
obtain the real fee schedule. Everything else on this register either
depends on one of these three or can proceed independently at the
school's own pace.

## Review cycle

Quarterly. Each review should move at least one `OPEN` item to `IN
PROGRESS` or `RESOLVED` — a register that never changes status is a
sign this document has stopped being used, not a sign nothing needs
doing.
