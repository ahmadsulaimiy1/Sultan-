# STATUTE OF THE MANAGEMENT TEAM

**Made under Article 163 of the Governance Charter of Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools, operationalising Chapter IV.**

**Status.** This Statute has been prepared for the Board of Governors' consideration and approval under Article 163 of the Governance Charter. It carries no legal effect until approved by the Board and recorded in the Authority Register.

**Classification.** Internal — this Statute concerns the Management Team's own internal operating procedure and is intended for circulation within the Institution's governance and executive structures, consistently with the internal-operations classification the Master Documentation Blueprint gives this document.

**Amendment note [2026-08-04].** This Statute, originally the Statute of the Executive Management Team, is retitled the Statute of the Management Team, per the Board-approved governance restructuring amendment of 2026-08-04 to the Governance Charter, which renamed the Executive Management Team the Management Team, restated its membership as the Head of Schools / Administrator and the Heads of the Institution's five Constituent Institutions (Governance Charter Article 51), and abolished the office of Chief Executive Officer in favour of the office of Head of Schools / Administrator. This Statute's own meeting, decision-making, and reporting procedure is otherwise unaffected, since it governs the body's process, not its membership.

---

## Section 1 — Citation, commencement, and authority

1.1. This instrument may be cited as the Statute of the Management Team.

1.2. This Statute is made by the Board of Governors under Article 163 of the Governance Charter.

1.3. This Statute takes effect on the date of its approval by the Board, recorded in the Authority Register under Article 16 of the Governance Charter.

1.4. This Statute operationalises Chapter IV of the Governance Charter (Articles 50–58) at the level of procedural detail Article 166 keeps out of the Charter's own text. It creates no power, office, or authority the Charter does not itself establish, per Article 163's own constraint, and is void to the extent of any inconsistency with the Charter, per Article 162.

1.5. Chapter IV contains no committee structure, supermajority threshold, or succession mechanism of its own for the Management Team to operationalise — the Management Team acts by consensus or, failing that, by the Head of Schools / Administrator's decision (Article 53), and continuity of the chair follows the Head of Schools / Administrator succession and acting-appointment provisions of Chapters XVI and XVII directly, not any separate Management Team-level mechanism. This Statute is correspondingly shorter than the Statute of the Board of Governors: it supplies the procedural detail Chapter IV's simpler design actually leaves open, not a parallel structure Chapter IV does not have.

## Section 2 — Definitions

2.1. Terms defined in Article 4 of the Governance Charter carry the same meaning in this Statute.

2.2. In this Statute, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "Meeting" means a meeting of the Management Team convened under Article 52;
(b) "member" means a person holding a seat on the Management Team under Article 51;
(c) "the Secretariat function" means the administrative role, held by a person the Head of Schools / Administrator designates, responsible for Management Team meeting logistics, papers circulation, and minute-keeping under this Statute — a Regulation-level administrative role, not itself a constitutional office, and distinct from (though it may in practice be held by the same person as) the Secretary function under the Statute of the Board of Governors.

## Section 3 — Meetings and papers

3.1. The Management Team meets not less than monthly, per Article 52. The Head of Schools / Administrator, or an Acting Head of Schools / Administrator designated under Article 25, convenes and chairs every Meeting.

3.2. The Head of Schools / Administrator settles the agenda for each Meeting. A member may propose an item for the agenda by notice to the Secretariat function not less than five days before the Meeting; the Head of Schools / Administrator includes a validly proposed item unless it more properly belongs before the Board, a Board Standing Committee, or another body under the Charter.

3.3. Papers shall be circulated to every member not less than three days before an ordinary Meeting, consistent with the shorter operational cadence Article 52's monthly meeting rhythm implies, and as far in advance as is practicable where a Meeting is convened on shorter notice to address an urgent operational matter.

## Section 4 — Conduct of business and decision-making

4.1. The Management Team ordinarily acts by consensus, per Article 53. Where consensus cannot be reached on a matter, the person chairing the Meeting hears every member's position before deciding, and records the decision and the reasons for it in the minutes, per Article 53.

4.2. A decision so taken binds every member in their dealings with staff and the public under Article 54, notwithstanding any member's private disagreement, provided that disagreement is itself recorded in the minutes at the time of the decision; a member who does not record disagreement at the time may not later treat the decision as other than binding on the ground of private reservation.

4.3. A Meeting may be held wholly or partly by durable, real-time electronic means, on the same footing, and subject to the same connectivity-confirmation duty on the Secretariat function, as Section 4 of the Statute of the Board of Governors provides for Board meetings — repeated here rather than cross-referenced only, so that this Statute is complete on its own reading for Management Team members who may not hold a copy of the Board's Statute.

4.4. Where a matter before the Management Team falls within the Academic Council's exclusive constitutional remit under Chapter VI, or within the Principal, Qur'an College's or Principal, Islamiyyah College's exclusive constitutional remit under Chapter VII, the Management Team does not decide it; Article 18 or Article 19, as applicable, governs how the Head of Schools / Administrator's oversight interacts with that remit instead.

## Section 5 — Minutes and records

5.1. The Secretariat function prepares a draft minute of each Meeting, recording: attendance (including by electronic means, per Section 4.3); each matter decided; whether decided by consensus or by the chairing person's decision under Section 4.1; any reasons recorded for a non-consensus decision; and any dissent recorded under Section 4.2.

5.2. The draft minute is circulated to every member who attended within seven days of the Meeting, and confirmed, with any correction, at the next Meeting.

5.3. Once confirmed, the minute is filed in the institutional archive under Article 134 of the Governance Charter, on the same append-only footing as the Board's own minutes under the Statute of the Board of Governors.

## Section 6 — Reporting to the Board

6.1. The Head of Schools / Administrator reports to the Board on the Management Team's behalf not less than once each ordinary Board meeting, on institutional performance, risk, and any matter requiring Board approval, per Article 55.

6.2. The Secretariat function maintains a standing log of matters the Management Team has identified as requiring Board approval under Article 46 but not yet brought forward, so that the Article 55 report can confirm, each time, that nothing awaiting Board approval has been left off an agenda.

## Section 7 — Relationship with the Board and with individual Governors

7.1. The Management Team is accountable to the Board through the Head of Schools / Administrator, per Article 56. No individual Governor may instruct a Management Team member directly; an instruction, where constitutionally proper, passes through Board resolution to the Head of Schools / Administrator.

7.2. Where a Governor raises an operational question directly with a Management Team member outside a Board or committee process, the member shall note the request and refer it to the Head of Schools / Administrator before acting on it, unless the matter is plainly routine and within the member's own ordinary authority to answer.

## Section 8 — Management-Level Committees

8.1. The Management Team may establish, vary, or dissolve a Management-Level Committee under Article 58 and Chapter XI, by its own resolution recorded in the Authority Register, without constitutional amendment.

8.2. A resolution establishing a Management-Level Committee shall state its purpose, membership, chair, and reporting line to the Management Team, and shall be reviewed by the Management Team not less than once yearly to confirm the Committee's continued usefulness.

8.3. This Statute does not itself constitute any Management-Level Committee; Section 8 states only the procedure by which the Management Team exercises the power Article 58 already gives it.

## Section 9 — Relationship to other instruments

9.1. This Statute is subordinate to the Governance Charter and, in particular, does not derogate from Articles 50–58.

9.2. This Statute should be read alongside the Management Team Charter once that document exists (Phase Two of the Institution's documentation programme); that Charter is expected to address the Management Team's substantive executive functions and individual role descriptions, while this Statute governs the Management Team's own meeting and decision-making procedure. Neither supersedes the other's distinct subject matter.

## Section 10 — Amendment and review

10.1. This Statute may be amended by the Board by the ordinary majority required for a Statute under Article 163.

10.2. This Statute shall be reviewed not less than once every two years, on a cycle aligned with the Statute of the Board of Governors so the two instruments can be reviewed together where convenient.

## Section 11 — Interpretation

11.1. This Statute is interpreted consistently with Article 9 of the Governance Charter. Where a genuine ambiguity arises that this Statute's own terms do not resolve, the Board resolves the question under Article 155.

## Drafting & audit record

**Legal audit.** Every Charter cross-reference (Articles 4, 9, 16, 18, 19, 25, 46, 50, 51, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56, 57, 58, 95, 134, 155, 162, 163, 166) was checked against the current Charter text before this Statute was considered complete. Article 57 (Head of Schools / Administrator may overrule any Management Team position other than a matter within another office's exclusive remit under Chapter VI or VII, in which case Article 18 or 19 applies) was checked specifically against Section 4.4's carve-out for Academic Council, Principal Qur'an College, and Principal Islamiyyah College remit, and the two are consistent: Section 4.4 doesn't add a new limit on Management Team authority, it restates, at the procedural level, the same boundary Article 57 already draws at the constitutional level. Article 25 was checked to confirm "Acting Head of Schools / Administrator" is the correct term Section 4.3's cross-reference and Section 3.1 both use, rather than an invented title.

**Governance audit.** Checked against Article 163's constraint that a Statute create no new power: Section 8's Management-Level Committee procedure doesn't grant the Management Team a new power, it only adds a resolution-content and annual-review requirement to the power Article 58 already gives it. Section 4.2's proviso — that a member must record disagreement at the time to preserve it — was checked against Article 54's own text ("notwithstanding any member's private disagreement recorded in the minutes") and found to track it precisely rather than narrow it: Article 54 already conditions the preserved-disagreement exception on the disagreement being "recorded," so Section 4.2 states explicitly what Article 54 already implies about timing, rather than adding a new condition Article 54 doesn't contain.

**Editorial audit.** Checked for terms used without definition (none), for consistency between this Statute's own Section numbering and its internal cross-references (confirmed), and for unnecessary duplication of the Statute of the Board of Governors' text — Section 4.3 duplicates that Statute's virtual-meeting provision deliberately, stated in Section 4.3 itself, rather than leaving a Management Team member to hunt for it in a different document; every other provision is Management Team-specific and not a restatement of the Board Statute.

**Publication audit.** Same standard and same deferred-typesetting decision as the other two Statutes in this phase (see the Phase One planning note, Section 6). Classification: Internal, consistent with this Statute's coverage of the Management Team's own operating procedure rather than public-facing governance structure (contrast the Statute of the Board of Governors, classified Public) or personal financial matters (contrast the Statute of the Founder's Reserved Rights, classified Confidential).
