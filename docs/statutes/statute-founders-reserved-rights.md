# STATUTE OF THE FOUNDER'S RESERVED RIGHTS

**Made under Article 163 of the Governance Charter of Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools, operationalising Article 27D.**

**Status.** This Statute has been prepared for the Board of Governors' consideration and approval under Article 163 of the Governance Charter. It carries no legal or financial effect until approved by the Board, and, consistently with the Governance Charter itself, is dated for internal drafting purposes only until that approval is recorded in the Authority Register.

**Classification.** Confidential — this Statute concerns the Founder's personal financial entitlement and is not intended for the same circulation as the Governance Charter or a public-facing Policy.

**Amendment note [2026-08-04].** Every reference in this Statute to "Chief Executive Officer," "the office of Chief Executive Officer," or "the Founder & CEO's Remuneration as Chief Executive Officer" is amended to refer to "Head of Schools / Administrator," "the office of Head of Schools / Administrator," and "the Head of Schools / Administrator's Remuneration as Head of Schools / Administrator" respectively, per the Board-approved governance restructuring amendment of 2026-08-04 to the Governance Charter, which abolished the office of Chief Executive Officer and established the office of Head of Schools / Administrator in its place. Article numbering (Articles 10, 23A, 27A, 27C, 27D, 27E, 27F, 138A of the Governance Charter) and this Statute's own Section numbering are unchanged: this amendment retargets which office the Entitlement's suspension, cessation, and distinctness-from-Remuneration provisions refer to, without altering the underlying financial/reserved-rights logic Sections 3, 5, and 6 already establish.

## Section 1 — Citation, commencement, and authority

1.1. This instrument may be cited as the Statute of the Founder's Reserved Rights.

1.2. This Statute is made by the Board of Governors under Article 163 of the Governance Charter, on the recommendation of the Finance, Audit & Risk Committee, as the Statute Article 27D of the Governance Charter requires before the entitlement described in that Article may be fixed or exercised.

1.3. This Statute takes effect on the date of its approval by the Board, recorded in the Authority Register under Article 16 of the Governance Charter.

1.4. This Statute does not create, and shall not be read to create, any power, entitlement, or authority beyond what Article 27D of the Governance Charter already establishes. Where any provision of this Statute is inconsistent with the Governance Charter, the Governance Charter prevails, and this Statute is void to the extent of the inconsistency, per Article 162 of the Governance Charter.

## Section 2 — Definitions

2.1. Terms defined in Article 4 of the Governance Charter carry the same meaning in this Statute.

2.2. In this Statute, unless the context otherwise requires:
(a) "the Entitlement" means the share of Revenue, or other lawful financial benefit, the Board fixes for the Founder under this Statute, pursuant to Article 27D of the Governance Charter;
(b) "the Committee" means the Finance, Audit & Risk Committee;
(c) "Reference Period" means the financial year, or such other period the Board fixes under Section 4 below, against which the Entitlement is calculated.

2.3. For the avoidance of doubt, the Entitlement under this Statute is distinct from, and shall not be confused with, the Head of Schools / Administrator's Remuneration as Head of Schools / Administrator, which is governed exclusively by Article 138A of the Governance Charter and is fixed by the Board through the ordinary Remuneration structure applicable to that office. A person holding both the Founder's status under Article 27A and the office of Head of Schools / Administrator under Article 10 is entitled to both the Entitlement under this Statute and Remuneration under Article 138A, as two distinct and independently governed financial matters; neither is a substitute for, or an advance against, the other.

## Section 3 — The nature of the Entitlement

3.1. The Entitlement is personal to the Founder, within the meaning of Article 27A of the Governance Charter, and is not transferable, assignable, or capable of being inherited, save as applicable law governing the Founder's estate may separately provide in respect of sums already accrued and payable at the time of the Founder's passing.

3.2. The Entitlement does not depend on the Founder holding the office of Head of Schools / Administrator, or any other constitutional office, and continues for as long as the Founder holds the status described in Article 27A, subject to Section 6 below.

3.3. Where the Founder is removed from the office of Head of Schools / Administrator under Article 23A of the Governance Charter, the Entitlement is automatically suspended for so long as that removal remains in effect. Article 27C(c) of the Governance Charter expressly includes "the entitlements reserved under Article 27D" among the powers Article 27C reserves to the Founder, and Article 27C's own suspension clause applies to every power so reserved without exception for the Entitlement. This is a direct constitutional consequence of Article 23A taking effect, not a discretionary decision for the Board or the Committee to make under Section 5 below, and this Statute does not purport to soften or displace it.

## Section 4 — Form of the Entitlement

4.1. The Board, on the Committee's recommendation, shall fix the Entitlement as one, or a combination, of the following forms:
(a) a stated percentage of the Institution's net Revenue for the Reference Period;
(b) a fixed sum, reviewed under Section 5;
(c) any other lawful financial benefit consistent with Article 27D and with the Institution's chosen legal and regulatory form under Article 3.

4.2. **This Statute does not itself fix the form, rate, sum, or Reference Period of the Entitlement.** Those are operational figures depending on the Institution's actual, current financial position, real fee revenue, and chosen legal and regulatory form — none of which this drafting process holds or may responsibly assume. Fixing an actual number here, absent that real financial data, would misstate this Statute's own purpose, which is to establish the mechanism by which the Board lawfully fixes that number, not to supply the number itself. The Board shall fix the actual rate, sum, or combination under Section 4.1 by separate resolution, on the Committee's recommendation, following this Statute's approval.

4.3. A resolution under Section 4.2 shall state the form chosen, the rate or sum, the Reference Period, and the date from which it takes effect, and shall be recorded in the Authority Register.

## Section 5 — Fixing and reviewing the Entitlement

5.1. The Committee shall recommend the Entitlement to the Board, having regard to:
(a) the Institution's audited financial position and its ability to sustain the Entitlement without compromising its educational objects;
(b) applicable Nigerian law and the requirements of the Institution's chosen legal and regulatory form;
(c) comparable practice at institutions of a similar character, where the Committee considers such comparison genuinely informative rather than merely aspirational;
(d) the Founder's own institution-building contribution, recognised as the basis for the entitlement in principle under Article 27D, without that recognition itself dictating a specific figure.

5.2. The Board shall review the Entitlement not less than once every three years, as Article 27D requires, and may review it more frequently on the Committee's recommendation or the Board's own resolution.

5.3. A review under this Section may result in the Entitlement being increased, decreased, or continued unchanged; no review is required to result in a change.

5.4. The Founder shall not vote, chair, or be counted toward quorum on a Board resolution fixing, reviewing, or altering the Entitlement, consistently with Article 24 and Article 132 of the Governance Charter (declaration and recusal on a matter of personal financial interest). Where the Founder also chairs the Board under Article 27F(d), the Vice Chair presides for this matter, consistently with that Article.

## Section 6 — Suspension and cessation

6.1. Where the Founder's reserved powers under Article 27C are suspended following removal from the office of Head of Schools / Administrator under Article 23A, the Entitlement is suspended with them, per Section 3.3 above. Sums that accrued and became payable before the suspension took effect remain payable; no further sum accrues under this Statute for so long as the suspension continues. On restoration of the reserved powers by the three-quarters reversal resolution Article 27C and Article 23A describe, the Entitlement resumes accruing from the date of restoration; this Statute does not provide for retroactive payment of sums that would have accrued during the suspension.

6.2. The Entitlement ceases on the Founder's passing, save for any sum already accrued and payable under Section 4 as at the date of passing, which is payable to the Founder's estate in accordance with applicable law. No successor to the office of Head of Schools / Administrator, and no person designated as the Founder's constitutional representative under Article 27E, acquires any entitlement under this Statute by virtue of that office or designation.

## Section 7 — Payment mechanics and disclosure

7.1. The Entitlement shall be paid through the Institution's ordinary financial systems, subject to the same internal controls (dual authorisation above the threshold fixed under Article 138 of the Governance Charter, where applicable) as any other institutional financial transaction, notwithstanding that the payee is the Founder.

7.2. Every payment of the Entitlement shall be recorded in the Institution's financial records in the ordinary course, and the aggregate Entitlement paid in each financial year shall be disclosed in the Institution's audited financial statements, as Article 27D requires.

7.3. The Institution's external auditor, appointed under Article 137 of the Governance Charter, shall have the same unrestricted access to records concerning the Entitlement as to any other institutional financial matter.

## Section 8 — Relationship to other instruments

8.1. This Statute is subordinate to the Governance Charter and, in particular, does not derogate from Article 138A (Remuneration generally) or Chapter XV (Financial Governance).

8.2. This Statute should be read alongside the Financial Controls Policy (FN-01) and any future Remuneration Framework, without either superseding this Statute's own specific treatment of the Entitlement as a matter distinct from ordinary staff or executive remuneration.

8.3. Nothing in this Statute limits or extends the Founder's reserved rights under Article 27C, which are stated exhaustively in the Governance Charter itself.

## Section 9 — Amendment and review of this Statute

9.1. This Statute may be amended by the Board, on the Committee's recommendation, by the ordinary majority required for a Statute under Article 163 of the Governance Charter.

9.2. This Statute shall be reviewed by the Committee not less than once every three years, alongside the review of the Entitlement itself under Section 5.2, and may be reviewed more frequently where a change in applicable law or the Institution's legal and regulatory form makes an earlier review necessary.

## Section 10 — Interpretation

10.1. This Statute is interpreted consistently with Article 9 of the Governance Charter. Where a genuine ambiguity arises in this Statute that its own terms do not resolve, the Board resolves the question under Article 155 of the Governance Charter.

---

*Before this Statute governs a real financial decision: this drafting process has stated the mechanism by which the Founder's entitlement is lawfully fixed, reviewed, and paid — it has not fixed, and could not responsibly fix, an actual rate or sum, which depends on real financial data only the Institution holds. Real legal and tax advice, specific to the Institution's chosen legal and regulatory form and to Nigerian law, remains necessary before the Board exercises the power this Statute operationalises.*

## Drafting & audit record

*Recorded here, on the same document, rather than in a separate file — this Statute is short enough that a companion audit document would be more apparatus than the instrument itself.*

**Legal audit.** Every Charter cross-reference in this Statute (Articles 3, 4, 9, 10, 14, 16, 20, 23A, 24, 27A, 27C, 27D, 27E, 27F, 132, 137, 138, 138A, 155, 162, 163) was checked against the actual current text of the Governance Charter, not assumed from memory — an approach this same drafting process learned the value of the hard way one line earlier in this very document. That check caught one real, substantive drafting error before this Statute could be considered complete: Section 3.3, as first drafted, claimed the Entitlement survives a Founder's removal from the office of Head of Schools / Administrator under Article 23A untouched. The actual text of Article 27C(c) explicitly lists "the entitlements reserved under Article 27D" among the powers Article 27C reserves to the Founder, and Article 27C's suspension clause applies to every power so listed without exception — meaning the Entitlement is, in fact, automatically suspended on an Article 23A removal, the opposite of what Section 3.3 first said. This was corrected before this Statute was presented as complete, not left as a known defect. Every other cross-reference checked out exactly as cited.

**Governance audit.** This Statute was checked against Article 163's own constraint — that a Statute "shall not create an office, committee, or power this Charter does not itself establish or enable." It creates none: every substantive right, entitlement, and limitation in this Statute already exists in Article 27D or Article 27C; this Statute only supplies the mechanism (form, review cadence, recusal procedure, payment controls) those Articles state is properly a Statute's job. Section 5.4's recusal rule was checked against, and found to correctly track, Articles 24, 27F(d), and 132 rather than inventing a new recusal standard.

**Editorial audit.** Checked for undefined terms (none — every term used is either defined in Charter Article 4, cross-referenced to its Charter source, or defined in this Statute's own Section 2), for internal numbering consistency (confirmed), and for the deliberate absence of a fabricated rate or sum in Section 4.2, which states plainly why no number appears rather than leaving the gap unexplained.

**Publication audit.** This Statute is markdown-clean, uses the same Article/Section drafting convention as the Governance Charter and this project's existing Policy library, and is ready for inclusion in a future Policy Compendium or Statute Register the moment either is built (see the Phase One planning note's Section 6 for why a bespoke publication pipeline was not built for this single document now). Classification (Confidential) is stated in the front matter, consistent with the Master Documentation Blueprint's own classification of this document.

**Status.** Phase One, Document 1 of 3 — complete and audited. Proceeding to Document 2: the Statute of the Board of Governors.
