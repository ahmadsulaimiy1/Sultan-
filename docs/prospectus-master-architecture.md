# SHRS Flagship Prospectus — Master Architecture v1.0

*Deliverable #2 of 5, built directly against `docs/prospectus-editorial-bible.md`
("the Bible") — every page allocation, spread count, and structural
decision below traces to a specific Part of the Bible, not a fresh
invention. Two points the Bible left open (Part III's Chairman/Head of Schools / Administrator
question, Part XI's real-statistics requirement) are resolved here as
**working assumptions**, stated plainly as assumptions rather than
confirmed facts, so this Architecture can proceed without inventing
content it has no authority to invent. Either assumption reverses
cleanly if SHRS leadership decides otherwise — nothing downstream
depends on them being permanent.*

## Working assumptions (not Bible-confirmed facts)

1. **Leadership voice**: one Welcome Message, from the founder/Director
   (the only publicly-named leadership voice), not a separate Chairman
   message. If SHRS later names a Board Chairman for this purpose, the
   Opening Spread (below) gains a second half-spread; the architecture
   absorbs that without restructuring the rest of the document.
2. **Statistics/data spreads carry structural placeholders, not
   fabricated numbers.** Every spread below that would normally show a
   figure (enrolment count, pass rate, staff count, years of
   operation) is marked `[FIGURE PENDING — SHRS TO SUPPLY]` in the
   content plan. Deliverable #3 (Spread-by-Spread Blueprint) should not
   receive invented numbers to fill these slots; real figures come from
   SHRS, or the spread is redesigned around evidence this project
   already has (governance-document count, published policy count,
   real dated milestones), consistent with Bible Part IX's "evidence
   over adjectives" standard.
3. **Edition**: English-language edition with Arabic in
   display/ceremonial contexts only (crest, seal, pull-quotes) — the
   Bible's own Part IV recommendation, formally adopted here as the
   working plan. A full bilingual edition is named as Phase 2, not
   built into this Architecture's page count.

**44 pages / 22 spreads**, landing inside the Bible's Part III range
(32–64 pages) at the upper-middle of the range — enough to give the
Institutions (Bible Part III) genuinely equal individual spreads

Part III requires, without inflating to a size that dilutes the
restraint principle in Part IX. Final trim size and binding (a
production decision, not an editorial one) is deferred to Deliverable
#5.

### Why three sections get an extra page
Qur'an College, the Digital Campus, and Governance & Safeguarding each
carry a genuine diagram requirement named explicitly in the Bible (Part
VII's four confirmed diagrams: Hifz Journey, Digital Campus Ecosystem,
Admissions Process, Academic Progression) or the Bible's own
instruction that Governance needs disproportionate design investment
(Part III, Part VIII) — a diagram or infographic-led page is one page
distinct from its narrative page, not folded into it, so the diagram
has room to breathe per Part IX's restraint principle.

## The Institutions: enforcing equal weighting structurally

*This spread plan (spreads 6–9) covers the four Constituent Institutions
with real, already-published header-nav facts to build a data panel
from. The fifth, Sultan Hanafi Online & Distance Learning School,
recognised by the Board's governance restructuring amendment of
2026-08-04, has no established programme facts yet to populate an
equivalent spread — stated plainly here rather than fabricated, and
left for a future revision of this Architecture once SHRS supplies
them.*

Per Bible Part III's explicit requirement ("equally-weighted
pillars, not Royal College as primary with supplements"): spreads
6–9 use an **identical two-page template** —

- Page 1: full-bleed anchor photograph, institution name (Cinzel
  display), one "at a glance" data panel (ages, format, established
  date — all real, already-published facts per the header nav
  descriptors: Basic School "Ages 2–10 · Day"; Royal College "Ages
  10+ · Day · Est. 2021"; School of Islamic and Arabic Studies "All ages · Weekday
  & weekend"; Qur'an College "Day & Boarding")
- Page 2: narrative paragraph, one supporting image, one distinguishing
  feature callout

Qur'an College's third page (the Hifz Journey diagram) is the *only*
structural asymmetry among the four, and it exists because Qur'an
College is the one institution with a genuinely distinct, publishable
five-stage methodology the other three don't have an equivalent of —
not because it's ranked above the others. This is named explicitly so
Deliverable #3 doesn't read the extra page as favouritism.

## Content flow verification against the three reader journeys (Bible Part III)

- **Parent journey** (Welcome → Mission/Values → their child's
  institution → Facilities/Student Life → Admissions → Contact) —
  achievable in this allocation: spreads 2, 4, 6–9 (select one), 10–11,
  13, 18 form a complete sub-path without requiring the full
  cover-to-cover read.
- **Student-facing journey** (Student Life → relevant institution →
  Achievements → Admissions) — achievable via spreads 10, 6–9 (select
  one), 16, 13.
- **International/diaspora journey** (Heritage → Governance → Digital
  Campus → Admissions) — achievable via spreads 3, 14, 12, 13; this is
  the longest-weighted path in the document (Heritage 2pp + Governance
  3pp + Digital Campus 3pp + Admissions 2pp = 10 pages), consistent
  with Bible Part III's note that this reader carries the most
  evidentiary weight.
