Fees and support — and why we ask

Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools holds back a number of bursary places every year, and the Sultan Zakariya Hanafi Foundation supports families who need help with fees. The two questions below let the Bursar consider your family for that support without you having to ask for it.

These answers are not part of the admission decision. No child is ever admitted, refused, or placed differently because of what a family earns. The answers are read by the Bursar's office alone, are never shown to teaching staff, and are never used to compare one family with another. Both questions may be left blank, and there is a “prefer not to say” option on each.

It helps the Bursar pitch a payment plan you can actually keep to.

Qur’an, Arabic and Islamic formation

Sultan Hanafi welcomes Muslim and non-Muslim children alike, and none of this is required. It exists so that a child is placed in the right group on the first morning rather than the third week — there is no right answer, and a child who has memorised nothing is exactly as welcome as one who has memorised ten juz’.
