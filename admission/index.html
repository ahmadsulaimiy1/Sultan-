Admission

Ready to Start Your Journey

Take the first step towards an exceptional education — our admission process in twelve clear stages.

EnquiriesPotential parents/guardians approach the school authority.
Administrative OfficeRelevant personnel attend to enquiries and provide guidance.
Admission FormPay for and submit the form with required documents.
Contact DetailsProvide contact information for further communication.
Entrance ExamStudent sits for the entrance examination, test, and interview.
Result NotificationParent/guardian notified of the result for further processing.
Admission OfferOffered into the qualifying class based on performance.
Fee PaymentPay tuition and other fees upon receipt of admission notice.
Admission LetterIssued with a unique number used throughout the learner's stay.
Class Acceptance TicketReceived to access the relevant class.
Start of ClassesClasses begin upon presentation of the access ticket.
Settling InThe learner enjoys our modern educational infrastructure.

Documents Required

  • Birth Certificate
  • Passport Photograph (2 copies)
  • Report Sheet or Testimonial Certificate from previous school(s)
