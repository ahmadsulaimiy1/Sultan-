Guidelines for Third Term Examination Questions

Internal Memorandum · Ref: SHRC/AC/001 · From the Principal to All Educators · 15 May 2026

  • Multiple Choice Section: 60 MCQs (40, plus a Section C for practical, for skill/practical-based subjects — Trade, CCA, Home Economics, etc.).
  • Theory Section: minimum of 4 questions, answer 3.
  • MCQ options: (A)–(D) begin on a new line, properly aligned.
  • Font: Times New Roman, size 12.
  • Coverage: First, Second, and Third Term topics at an approximate 10:10:40 ratio.
  • Standardisation: SSS questions at WAEC/UTME standard as appropriate across classes.
  • Heading: the school's official, registered, and accredited name and logo — Sultan Hanafi Secular College (Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools) — plus Session 2025/2026, Third Term Examination.
  • Avoid polar questions (Yes/No, True/False).
  • File naming: a folder named for you, containing each subject as Subject_Class_TermPaper (e.g. Maths_JSS1_TTEXAM).
  • Submission: to your HoD, by the date on the Notable Dates schedule below.

All staff, including part-time and corps members, are expected to comply. — Academic Committee SHRC (ACSHRC, 2026)

Responsibilities of Teacher of Duty

  1. Prepares the attached student(s) on the week's topic and discusses it.
  2. Resumes school by 7:15 am.
  3. Welcomes both parents and students with a smile.
  4. Joins to conduct assembly: 7:30 – 8:00 am.
  5. Is the supervisor of the week: supervises students' activities and movement.
  6. Gives a weekly report on school activities and observations.
  7. Ensures students transit smoothly to the next section (Islaamiyyah).
  8. Monitors all school properties and ensures everything is in place.
  9. Closing time for teacher on duty is 3:30 pm, except Fridays and exam week.

These documents are circulated internally to staff and are not published on the public website — the same discipline the Role & Permission Matrix and Staff Conduct Policy apply everywhere else in this system. If a date or assignment here looks out of date, contact the Office of the Principal.
