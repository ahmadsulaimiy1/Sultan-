4 — Certificate Register & Revocation

Certificate Register

Search by serial number, student name, or Student ID. Every certificate can be re-opened, re-printed, publicly verified, or revoked (with a mandatory note) — revocation shows instantly at the public verification page; the serial is never reused.

Records Integrity

Issuing a batch is a permission-checked, audit-logged act of the Registrar's certificate authority under the SHRS Role & Permission Matrix. Serial numbers come from an atomic, never-reused sequence; each carries a cryptographic anti-forgery suffix; and every public verification of every serial is recorded in the verification log. If an action is refused and you believe it shouldn't be, contact the ICT Office at info@shroyalschools.com.
