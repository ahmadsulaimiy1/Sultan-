Nursery & Primary School

Sultan Hanafi Nursery & Primary School stands as a beacon of holistic education, nurturing young minds through a blend of secular and Islamic teachings. Early years education here is not just about imparting knowledge — it's about fostering a love for learning and exploration, led by Head Teacher Mrs. Kareemat Abdurazaq.

A Mixed Curriculum Practice

The Royal College and Basic School adopt the Nigerian National Curriculum for the secular programme, infused with entrepreneurial skills, financial intelligence, leadership, broad-based technology, personal development, career planning, and work-ready elective subjects.

Islamic & Qur'anic Studies

Our Islamic & Arabic School and Qur'an College adopt the Saudi Arabian educational curriculum, with Saudi-approved textbooks. Extra-curricular activities span sports & games, debate, and Islamic arts & culture.
