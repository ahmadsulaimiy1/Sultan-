Royal College

A comprehensive curriculum integrating junior and secondary education, encompassing both Islamic and secular studies. Led by Dr. Adegoke Musa Olatunji, the College fosters critical thinking, moral values, and a sense of responsibility across seven academic departments.

Languages
English, Yoruba, French (future), Hausa (future), Chinese (future)

Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Computer Studies, Data Processing, Programming/Coding
Humanities
Geography, History, Government, Civic Education, Art, Literature in English, Social Studies
Science & Technology
Biology, Physics, Chemistry, Agricultural Science, Food & Nutrition, Technical Drawing, Basic Technology, Home Economics, PHE
Commerce & Management
Financial Accounting, Commerce, Economics, Bookkeeping, Marketing, Business Studies
Arabic
Arabic, Nahwu & Sarfu (Grammar), Aruud (Poetry), Balaghah (Rhetoric), Al-Adab-Al Arabiy (Literature), Al-Inshaw (Composition)
Islamic Sciences

A Mixed Curriculum Practice

The Royal College and Basic School adopt the Nigerian National Curriculum for the secular programme, infused with entrepreneurial skills, financial intelligence, leadership, broad-based technology, personal development, career planning, and work-ready elective subjects.

Islamic & Qur'anic Studies

Our Islamic & Arabic School and Qur'an College adopt the Saudi Arabian educational curriculum, with Saudi-approved textbooks. Extra-curricular activities span sports & games, debate, and Islamic arts & culture.
