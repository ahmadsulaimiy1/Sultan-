School of Arabic & Islamic Studies

Established to cater to the educational needs of the Muslim Ummah, offering comprehensive Islamic studies, Arabic proficiency, and memorisation of the Qur'an and Hadith, led by Principal Shaykh Abubakr Solah.

Weekday ClassesMonday – Wednesday · 2:00pm – 6:00pm
Weekend ClassesSaturday & Sunday · 9:00am – 3:00pm

This programme is open to the wider community, not only to Sultan Hanafi's enrolled day and boarding students — attendance does not require going through the school's 12-step admission process.

Islamic & Qur'anic Studies

Our Islamic & Arabic School and Qur'an College adopt the Saudi Arabian educational curriculum, with Saudi-approved textbooks. Extra-curricular activities span sports & games, debate, and Islamic arts & culture.

A Mixed Curriculum Practice

The Royal College and Basic School adopt the Nigerian National Curriculum for the secular programme, infused with entrepreneurial skills, financial intelligence, leadership, broad-based technology, personal development, career planning, and work-ready elective subjects.
