Parent / Guardian Portal

The family's side of the institution, and the account most households need. Every family-facing step — an admission, a fee, a report, a record of memorisation — belongs to this one account rather than to four separate logins.

  • Admission status, from enquiry through to offer
  • Fees, payment history and verifiable receipts
  • Your child's academic record and Ḥifẓ progress
  • Announcements addressed to your family

Free to create, and open to any parent or guardian.

Applicant Portal

Where an admission begins. Apply is not a separate system: it is Stage 3 of the same admissions journey, which is why a free Parent/Guardian account comes first and everything submitted afterwards stays attached to it.

  • Stage 1 — create your Parent/Guardian account
  • Stage 2 — confirm the school and the class
  • Stage 3 — complete and submit the application

Begin here if you hold an account; otherwise create one first, at no cost.

Academic Staff Portal

The teaching side of the Digital Campus, for principals and teaching staff across every school in the group — the Royal College, the School of Islamic and Arabic Studies, the Qur'ān College, and the Nursery and Primary School.

  • Your classes, registers and timetable
  • Entering and releasing results
  • Academic leadership instruments for those who hold them

Requires a teaching credential issued by your school's administration.

Administrative Portal

One sign-in for every operational office the institution runs. You need not know in advance which office system to open: you sign in once, and an Office Switcher shows exactly which offices your account holds.

  • Registrar — enrolment, records, transcripts and credentials
  • Finance — fees, receipts and institutional accounts
  • Human Resources and the wider institutional services

Requires a staff credential. Permissions attach to the account, not to the door.

Enter the Administrative Portal →

Executive Portal

The institution-wide command centre, reserved for the offices accountable for the whole of Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools rather than for any single school within it.

  • Consolidated analytics across every school at once
  • Governance papers, council and committee records
  • Institution-level oversight of admissions, finance and academic standing

Requires an executive credential issued by the Institutional Administration Centre.

Enter the Executive Portal →

Public Verification Portal

The door that is deliberately open. An employer, a university or an examinations body may confirm that an SHRS document is genuine without ever holding an account here — the only arrangement under which a credential is worth anything to the person carrying it.

  • Certificates, transcripts and Ijāzah
  • Graduation documents
  • Student, guardian and staff identity cards
  • Fee receipts

Public, instant and free. You need only the reference number or the QR code.
