Where We Stand Today

One Board, Four Institutions

Founded in 2017, Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools today comprises four institutions — Nursery & Primary School, Royal College, the School of Islamic & Arabic Studies, and Qur'an College — governed by one Board of Trustees and one Chief Executive Officer. See Governance & Leadership for the full structure.

A Real Digital Campus, Already Live

A shared Parent Portal, Student Portal, Staff Identity system, and Founder Dashboard already serve real families and staff today — not a future promise. See the full Digital Campus chapter.

A Written Governance Framework, Under Adoption

A full suite of institutional policies — academic, safeguarding, financial, and data-protection — has been drafted and is progressing through Board review and adoption. See the Policies Centre for the current status of each.

What's In Development

The sections below are genuinely being worked on — but none has a Board-approved target date or figure yet. Rather than publish an invented "Vision 2030" with numbers no one has actually agreed to, this page will be updated with real, adopted targets once the Board sets them.
