Activation of Your Official Portal Account — Office of the Registrar

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ

Respected Madam,

Assalāmu ‘alaykum wa raḥmatullāhi wa barakātuh. I pray this letter meets you and your household in good health and in the safekeeping of Allāh.

I write on behalf of the ICT Office to inform you that your official account on the Sultan Hanafi Royal Schools Digital Campus has been created, and that the Office of the Registrar is now open to you online.

Your Staff Identity Number is SHRS-HQ-REG-130826-000004. This number is issued by the institution itself, is permanent, and will identify you on every record, certificate, transcript and verification the school produces for as long as you hold office. It does not change if your office, campus or title changes. Please keep it safely; you will be asked for it.

What you need to do

1. Open your activation link. https://shroyalschools.com/portal/staff/set-password/?token=DEMO

shroyalschools.com

Every address in this letter is a live link. If you are reading this letter on a telephone or computer, you do not need to copy anything out or type any address by hand: touch or click the underlined text and it will open. The activation link above, the sign-in address below, and this office’s e-mail address all behave in the same way. If you are reading a printed copy, the addresses are written out in full so that they may be typed exactly as they appear.

This link belongs to you alone. It may be used once, and it expires seven days from the date of this letter. Should it lapse before you reach it, nothing whatever is lost — write to the ICT Office and a fresh link will be issued the same day.

2. Choose your password. The link opens a page asking you to set one. It must be at least ten characters. Please choose something only you would think of, and please do not reuse a password you already use elsewhere.

No one at this school sets your password, and no one can see it — not the ICT Office, not the Head of Schools, not the system administrator. This is deliberate. It means that if you forget it, nobody can simply tell it to you; you will instead be sent a fresh link to choose a new one. It also means that any message asking you to disclose your password did not come from this school, whatever it appears to say.

3. Sign in. Go to shroyalschools.com/portal/staff/login/ and sign in with your email address and the password you have just chosen. For your protection a one‑time code may be sent to your email at sign‑in; it is valid for ten minutes, and you have five attempts before a fresh code must be requested.

What your office now opens

Your portal carries the authority of the Registrar across all five institutions — the Nursery & Primary School, Royal College, the School of Islamic & Arabic Studies, the Qur’an College, and the Online & Distance Learning School. Within it you will find:

The Registrar’s Office — admissions verification, enrolment, results, transcripts and certificates, across every institution rather than one at a time.

The Newsroom — where the school’s announcements are written and published. What you publish there appears on the website and reaches every family who has asked to be notified.

Your Desk — where families who have asked to speak with a person, rather than with the digital assistant, are brought to your attention, together with notifications addressed to you by name.

Examination readiness — the WAEC and NECO oversight views.

Delegation — should you travel or take leave, you may pass your authority to a named colleague for a stated period, with your reason recorded. It ends by itself on the date you set; you need not remember to withdraw it, and you may end it earlier at any time.

A note on the record

Every action taken in the portal is recorded against the person who took it. This is not surveillance. It is the ordinary discipline of an institution that issues certificates and holds children’s records, and it protects you at least as much as it protects the school: where the record shows who did what and when, no officer can be asked to answer for another’s decision.

It is also the reason your account is yours alone and must never be shared with a colleague, however convenient it may seem in a busy week. An action taken on your account is recorded as yours.

If anything does not work

Please do not attempt a workaround. Write to the ICT Office at info@shroyalschools.com, stating what you were doing and the exact wording that appeared on the screen. A message quoting the screen word for word is resolved far more quickly than one that describes it.

Kindly confirm to this office once you have signed in successfully. The ICT Office remains at your disposal, and I look forward to the good work of your office, now properly supported.

What shroyalschools.com now is

It may help your office to know what has changed, because the difference is one of kind and not of appearance.

What the school had before was a website, published at shroyalschools.ng: pages that described the school to a visitor. It presented information, and when the information changed somebody edited a page. It held nothing, decided nothing and proved nothing — no record lived inside it, no decision passed through it, and no document it displayed could be verified by anyone who doubted it.

That address has been retired, and the school’s entire presence now sits under shroyalschools.com. The old address is written here once, in the past tense, only so that your office is in no doubt which site is meant. It should no longer be quoted to parents, printed on any document, or given out in correspondence; where it still appears on older printed material, kindly have it corrected to the .com address.

What shroyalschools.com now carries is a Digital Campus: the same public pages, and behind them a system of record. It holds the students’ records, the guardians’ records, admissions, attendance, assessments, results, report cards, Ḥifẓ and Ijāzah records, graduation and its clearances, certificates and transcripts. It knows who each member of staff is, what office they hold, and what that office may and may not do — and it keeps a record of what each person did and when.

Three consequences follow, and they are the reason this work was undertaken:

A document issued by the school can be checked by anyone. Every certificate and transcript carries a verification address. An employer, a university or a ministry may confirm it without telephoning this school and without trusting a photocopy. That protects our graduates, and it protects the school’s name from anyone who would forge it.

Authority is held by office, not by password. What you may do, you may do because you are the Registrar. Should you leave the office, the authority leaves with the office and not with you; should you travel, you may lend it for a stated period and it returns by itself. No one holds a general key to everything.

The record defends the person who kept it. Corrections are logged with the name of whoever made them. This is not surveillance. It is the ordinary discipline of an institution that issues certificates and holds children’s records, and on the day a mark or a date is disputed it is the Registry’s protection before it is anybody else’s.

The pages the public sees are also now in four languages — English, Arabic, Yorùbá and French — because a school of this character is read by more than one kind of reader.

Also now open to your office

Since your account was prepared, several parts of the Digital Campus that belong to the Registry have been completed. They are listed here so that your office knows what is available rather than discovering it piecemeal.

The Graduation Control Centre. Graduation is no longer a single approval but a recorded chain, and your office begins it. When you mark a graduating record under review or verified, that opens the clearance chain; the Principal of the candidate’s own institution then acts at the principal stage, and you retain visibility of the whole chain from one screen. Nothing further in the chain can move until the Registry has acted, which is the correct order and is now enforced by the system rather than by memory.

The Certificate Generation Centre. Certificates and transcripts are generated from the record rather than typed, and each carries a verification address that any employer, university or ministry may check. Your office creates certificates once graduation is approved, and creates and issues transcripts in its own right.

Your digital signature. This is the one item that requires an action from you before certificates and transcripts can be issued at all. Documents name their required signatories, and the Registrar is named on the certificate, the transcript and the diploma supplement. If a required signatory has no signature on file, generation stops with a named vacancy rather than printing a blank signature line. Under My Signature in the portal you may upload a scan of your own signature; you manage only your own, and no one else — including this office — can upload it for you.

Examination readiness. WAEC and NECO readiness is held jointly by your office and Academic Affairs, and you may create and correct entries there.

Admissions verification. Applications reach you for verification and for the waiting list; your office approves at that stage.

Corrections, and the record of them. Where attendance or an assessment is wrong, your office may correct it. Every such correction is logged against your name. That is deliberate: a registry that can silently alter a mark is not a registry, and the log is what allows a correction to be defended years later.

The Newsroom. School-wide academic notices are written, published and archived by your office, and reach the website and every family who has asked to be notified.

Delegation. If you travel or take leave, you may pass your authority to a named colleague for a stated period, with a reason recorded. It ends by itself on the date you set; you need not remember to withdraw it.

What this office suggests you do first

Sign in, upload your signature, and open the Graduation Control Centre once to see the chain as it stands. Those three take a few minutes together and they unblock everything else. Nothing else needs your attention on the first day.

Work still in hand

Kindly stay in touch with this office. The team continues to build, and we will report to you and to the Board at intervals — not only the developments that succeed, but the difficulties met and the areas in which we know the work is not yet good enough. An office that reports only its successes cannot be relied upon, and we do not intend to be that office.

Should anything be unclear, or should the portal behave in a way you do not expect, please write to this office and say plainly what you were doing and what appeared on the screen. No question is too small, and you will not be thought less capable for asking it.

May Allāh grant your office tawfīq, and make this work a benefit to our students and a ṣadaqah jāriyah for all who have laboured over it.
